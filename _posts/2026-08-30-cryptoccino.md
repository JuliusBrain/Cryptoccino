## Cosmos Labs Concedes It Wrongly Closed the Bug That Let Attackers Hit Six Chains for $5.7M
{: #cosmos-labs-concedes-it-wrongly-closed-the-bug-that-let-attackers-hit-six-chains-for-57m}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-08-29-cosmos-labs-says-it-wrongly-cleared-the-bug-behind-a-5-7-million-six-chain-hack-413061)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">69<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+3 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Cosmos Labs has acknowledged it incorrectly marked as resolved the vulnerability that was subsequently exploited across six IBC-connected chains, draining a combined $5.7 million. MANTRA Chain, which absorbed the largest individual loss at $3.6 million, says the actual patch landed only 20 hours before the attack began and that the fix never identified which flaw it was closing.

**Why it matters.** The incident is structurally different from a garden-variety smart-contract drain: the bug lived at the shared infrastructure layer, which means every chain running that version of the Cosmos SDK carried exposure simultaneously. A wrongly-cleared ticket in a public bug tracker gave operators a false all-clear, compressing the effective remediation window to near zero.

**The catch.** The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 69 (Greed) with the seven-day trend still nudging higher, meaning the broader market is priced for calm precisely when shared-infrastructure risk is demonstrably not contained. Operators running IBC-connected validators should audit which SDK version their chain is on and verify that the patch in question is actually deployed, not just logged as done.
