**What happened.** The White House backed ethics language for the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act that would bar federal officials from issuing cryptocurrencies and hand enforcement to the DOJ. Polymarket odds on the bill passing in 2026 jumped to around 43-45%, and Bitcoin briefly broke above $66,000 to a seven-week high on the news.

**Why it matters.** The ethics provisions were the central Democratic sticking point, so White House sign-off is a genuine unlock. Treasury Secretary Bessent separately urged Congress to move the bill, signalling coordinated executive-branch pressure. With Fear &amp; Greed sitting at 33, the market's muted response tells you traders aren't pricing a done deal.

**The catch.** Senate Democrats are still publicly balking, calling the ethics terms insufficient. The bill also remains tangled in the broader legislative calendar alongside the stablecoin bill. Passing odds near 45% means the market is roughly coin-flipping on whether this clears before year-end.
