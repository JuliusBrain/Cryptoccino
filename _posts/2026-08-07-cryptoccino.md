## Coldcard's software bug lets attackers drain cold wallets over days, shaking the last line of hardware defence.
{: #coldcards-software-bug-lets-attackers-drain-cold-wallets-over-days-shaking-the-last-line-of-hardware-defence}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-06/bitcoin-btc-faithful-tested-as-hackers-drain-funds-stashed-offline-msi84uao) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-06/bitcoin-sleuths-are-going-sleepless-after-coldcard-wallet-hack) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/hacked-bitcoin-wallet-maker-declines-to-estimate-amount-lost) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/coinkite-coldcard-bitcoin-hack-130m/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Coinkite's Coldcard hardware wallets were compromised via a software bug, enabling attackers to siphon funds from devices that were supposed to be air-gapped. The theft played out over multiple days before the broader community noticed, and on-chain researchers have been working through the weekend to scope the losses. Coinkite has declined to put a number on customer losses and says a formal post-mortem is in preparation.

**Why it matters.** Cold storage is the bedrock assumption that lets large holders sleep at night. A multi-day, software-triggered drain of ostensibly offline devices breaks that mental model in a way that a hot-wallet exploit does not. The incident lands while sentiment is already reading 29 on the Fear &amp; Greed Index, adding a credibility wound to an already fragile market.

**The catch.** Total losses remain unconfirmed, which is itself part of the problem: the uncertainty is fuelling speculation well beyond any verified figure. Coinkite saying it will publish a post-mortem "eventually" is not the same as incident containment. Until the attack surface is fully characterised, every Coldcard user is running a risk they cannot properly size.
