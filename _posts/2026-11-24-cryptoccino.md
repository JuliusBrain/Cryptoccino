## Bitcoin's Dead-Cat Bounce Dies on Arrival as ETF Exodus Continues.
{: #bitcoins-dead-cat-bounce-dies-on-arrival-as-etf-exodus-continues}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-24/bitcoin-fails-to-hold-nvidia-bump-with-crypto-headwinds-in-focus) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/bitcoin-etf-exodus-gathers-pace-removing-key-source-of-support) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/23/bitcoin-rebounds-from-2-year-low-as-wall-street-futures-recover) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383758/bitcoin-rebounds-to-61k-after-weekend-plunge-to-two-year-low) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-23-grayscale-bitcoin-altcoins-oversold-ai-risks-crypto-outlook-445418)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">11<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slid to a two-year low near $55,000 over the weekend, then briefly clawed back above $61,000 on the Nvidia-Jensen Huang news before surrendering nearly all of those gains. Spot ETFs logged their largest single-day outflow since June, and the losing streak is now the second-longest on record, at least 411 days by Grayscale's count.

**Why it matters.** The failure to hold even a 30%-Nasdaq-day bounce signals the crypto bid is structurally weak, not just sentiment-driven. The Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 11, Extreme Fear, and has barely moved in a week, suggesting this isn't a panic spike but a grinding re-rating. ETF outflows removing the marginal buyer that propped up the 2024 rally is the mechanism to watch.

**The catch.** Grayscale calls the asset class oversold and flags AI-sector risk as the near-term overhang rather than any crypto-native problem. Strategy's enterprise value has now dropped below the mark-to-market value of its 851,305 BTC stack, a signal that Saylor's premium is gone even as he bought another 457 BTC at current levels. The five-year return on bitcoin is effectively flat, which breaks the core CAGR narrative that brought institutional capital in.
