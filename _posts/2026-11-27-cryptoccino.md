## Binance Declares Withdrawals Fully Restored, Four Weeks After a $6.3 Billion Hack
{: #binance-declares-withdrawals-fully-restored-four-weeks-after-a-63-billion-hack}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/27/binance-says-its-withdrawal-suspension-is-over-four-weeks-after-a-usd6-3-billion-hack) [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/uk-fca-defi-regulation-binance-hack) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/384027/uk-regulator-cites-binance-hack-as-it-plans-tougher-defi-rules-than-us-and-eu-report)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">12<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Binance confirmed that all customer withdrawals are operational again, four weeks after a $6.3 billion exploit froze the platform. The exchange had suspended withdrawals while it assessed damage, restructured custody flows, and cooperated with investigators tracing the funds.

**Why it matters.** The incident is now the load-bearing justification for a wave of DeFi regulatory tightening: North Korea-linked actors reportedly used DeFi protocols to launder the stolen funds, and the UK's FCA is explicitly citing the hack as grounds for proposed rules stricter than anything in the US or EU. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 12, Extreme Fear, with no recovery in seven days suggests markets have not priced in a clean resolution here.

**Watch.** US prosecutors have reportedly moved to recover more than $1.1 billion of the stolen crypto, but the bulk of the funds remain at large. The FCA's proposed framework would require DeFi platforms to seek authorisation and would bar regulated exchanges from routing UK retail flow to unauthorised protocols, a structural change that would redraw how post-hack laundering corridors work.
