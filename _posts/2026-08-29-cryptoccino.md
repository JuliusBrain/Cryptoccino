## Warsh's Jackson Hole Debut Pulls Bitcoin Below $80K After $580M ETF Inflow Day.
{: #warshs-jackson-hole-debut-pulls-bitcoin-below-80k-after-580m-etf-inflow-day}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/bitcoin-slips-below-80-000-after-kevin-warsh-inflation-vow) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/28/bitcoin-slides-below-78000-markets-digest-warsh-hawkish) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/376813/bitcoin-traders-watch-fed-chair-warsh-for-clues-and-get-nothing) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/institutions-580m-crypto-etfs-warsh-speech/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">68<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−3 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Fed Chair Kevin Warsh used his first Jackson Hole keynote to double down on the inflation fight, pushing short-term Treasury yields higher and sending Bitcoin from a three-month high back below $80,000. Institutions had poured roughly $580 million into crypto ETFs in the session before the speech, with ETH funds alone taking in $226 million, their strongest day in ten months.

**Why it matters.** The move confirms that macro rate expectations are still the short-term throttle for crypto risk. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 68 (Greed) but has shed three points over the week, a quiet reminder that the sentiment backdrop was already softening before Warsh opened his mouth.

**The catch.** Warsh offered zero forward guidance on September, so the selloff is as much a relief-rally unwind as a fundamental repricing. BTC is still outperforming equities on a weekly basis and the Coinbase premium has returned after a long absence, suggesting domestic spot demand hasn't evaporated.
