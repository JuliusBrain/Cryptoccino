## SEC Pulls the Plug on Reg Crypto Proposal and Shelves the Tokenisation Exemption — Again.
{: #sec-pulls-the-plug-on-reg-crypto-proposal-and-shelves-the-tokenisation-exemption-again}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/13/sec-cancels-long-awaited-proposal-of-reg-crypto-postponing-meeting-without-new-date) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/13/u-s-sec-to-again-delay-innovation-exemption-for-tokenization-amid-wall-street-white-house-concerns) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/sec-cancels-crypto-rules-meeting/) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/375547/morning-minute-the-sec-plans-rules-for-tokenized-stocks)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** The SEC cancelled a long-scheduled open meeting that was expected to formally propose Reg Crypto, the agency's framework for crypto asset regulation, without setting a new date. Separately, the Commission confirmed it is again delaying its innovation exemption for tokenised securities, citing pushback from both Wall Street firms and the White House.

**Why it matters.** Two simultaneous retreats from the same regulator in one day signals something beyond scheduling friction — internal disagreement, political pressure, or both. With the Fear &amp; Greed Index parked at 29 and flat for a week, the market is already in a wait-and-see crouch; losing the clearest near-term regulatory catalyst hardens that posture.

**The catch.** The CFTC is moving in the opposite direction, convening its Innovation Advisory Committee on 20 August to address crypto, AI and prediction market regulation without waiting for Congress to pass the CLARITY Act. The divergence between the two agencies is now a structural risk for any project trying to determine which regulator they actually answer to.
