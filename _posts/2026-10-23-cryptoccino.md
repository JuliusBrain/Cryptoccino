## AI Confidence Crack Pulls Bitcoin Below $82K, Wipes $383M in Longs and Sends Asian Equities Sliding.

**What happened.** A broad AI-stock selloff extended through Asian sessions, pulling Bitcoin from a brief $85K recovery back below $81,500. South Korea's Kospi fell 2.3%, Japan's Nikkei 225 dropped 1.6%, and China formally entered bear-market territory. DeFi tokens led the crypto slide while $383 million in leveraged longs were liquidated in 24 hours, burning traders on both sides of the week's round-trip.

**Why it matters.** The move confirms that Bitcoin is still reading as a risk asset in macro drawdowns, not a hedge. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 50, neutral, but the seven-day delta is minus 13, meaning the retreat in sentiment preceded this leg lower and has room to run. Global credit spreads are at a 17-month high on AI-capex anxiety, and CoreWeave's $35B refinancing stress is adding a credible tail risk to the sector that was propping up GPU demand narratives.

**The catch.** Spot Bitcoin ETFs pulled in $1.08 billion over the three preceding sessions and erased October's outflow deficit, so institutional demand has not evaporated. But Thursday's session saw $635 million leave ETFs, with BlackRock's IBIT leading the exit, which complicates the bullish flow narrative heading into the weekend. Until AI sentiment stabilises, macro-correlated selling is likely to cap any rally attempt.
