## Galaxy abandons its bitcoin bottom call as miners liquidate and veterans warn this sell-off is different.
{: #galaxy-abandons-its-bitcoin-bottom-call-as-miners-liquidate-and-veterans-warn-this-sell-off-is-different}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-26-galaxy-walks-back-bitcoin-bottom-call-446786) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-25-bitcoin-falls-below-58000-coinshares-butterfill-sees-tail-end-bear-market-446678) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383967/bitcoin-sinks-inflation-trump-tariffs-ai-selloff) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/25/this-time-actually-seems-different-crypto-veterans-brace-for-more-pain-as-bitcoin-tumbles) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/bitcoin-miners-hammered-again-as-price-rout-squeezes-margins)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">11<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slipped below $58,000 on Tuesday before clawing back to just above $60,000 after roughly $90 million in leveraged shorts were liquidated. Galaxy Research formally withdrew its prior call that June's close was the cycle bottom, now treating a daily close below $58,525 as confirmation of a materially lower move ahead. The catalyst mix was ugly: hotter PCE inflation, renewed tariff signals from the Trump administration, and a synchronised AI-stock selloff all hit at once.

**Why it matters.** The macro headwinds are compounding structural selling: listed miners are being forced to liquidate token reserves to cover operating costs and fund their AI data-centre pivots, adding persistent spot pressure that short squeezes can only absorb temporarily. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 11, Extreme Fear, with virtually no recovery from last week, suggesting the market is not yet pricing in a credible floor. Ether and DeFi tokens fell roughly twice as hard as bitcoin, confirming rotation out of risk-on crypto is broadening.

**The catch.** CoinShares' James Butterfill calls this the tail end of a bear market, not the beginning of a new one, and CoinShares' own data shows some institutional buyers treating dips as accumulation. The bounce off $58K was real, but it was short-driven, and the same June closing low that Galaxy just flagged as the last line of defence is now squarely in the market's sights.
