## Bitget Attacker Ran Test Transfers Before $388 Million Drain, THORChain Refuses to Help.
{: #bitget-attacker-ran-test-transfers-before-388-million-drain-thorchain-refuses-to-help}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-28-bitget-attacker-tested-risk-controls-small-transfers-388-million-theft-ceo-says-417045) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-28-bitget-starts-phased-withdrawal-resumption-416965) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/09/28/thorchain-rejects-bitget-request-to-block-hacker-as-usd6-million-moves-to-bitcoin)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">73<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−5 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitget CEO Gracy Chen confirmed the attacker probed the exchange's risk controls with two small test transfers roughly 30 minutes before executing the full $388 million theft. The exchange has since patched the vulnerability and begun a phased resumption of withdrawals, pledging to cover all losses from its user protection fund.

**Why it matters.** The reconnaissance pattern is textbook: small-value transfers to confirm controls are blind before the real drain. That it worked at an exchange of Bitget's scale points to gaps in real-time anomaly detection that any exchange security team should be pressure-testing today. The Fear &amp; Greed Index still sits at 73, meaning retail is not yet pricing in contagion risk from a nine-figure exchange breach.

**The catch.** Bitget asked THORChain to block the hacker's address after roughly $6 million had already bridged to Bitcoin, and THORChain's governance declined, citing its censorship-resistance principles. That refusal effectively confirms that once funds reach a permissionless cross-chain router, exchange-level recovery efforts are over. It is a live demonstration of why pre-bridge controls are the only controls that matter.
