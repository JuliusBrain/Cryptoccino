**BITCOIN GOVERNANCE**

## BIP-110 Minority Chain Breaks Off as Mainnet Miners Refuse to Play Along.
{: #bip-110-minority-chain-breaks-off-as-mainnet-miners-refuse-to-play-along}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-08-08-bitcoins-bip-110-supporters-split-onto-minority-chain-as-main-network-pulls-ahead-411213?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/08/07/frame-bitcoin-s-bip-110-enters-mandatory-signaling-with-less-than-3-miner-support) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bip-110-nodes-fork-bitcoin-chain-split/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">31<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** At block 961,632, BIP-110's mandatory signalling window opened. With fewer than 3% of miners signalling support, nodes backing the proposal rejected a non-signalling block from the main network and split onto a separate minority chain. The main chain is pulling ahead by every meaningful metric.

**Why it matters.** BIP-110 would have placed a one-year restriction on non-financial data inscribed in Bitcoin blocks, making it the most contentious governance fight since SegWit. The split demonstrates that a well-organised minority can force a chain separation even without economic weight behind it, which sets a precedent for future contested soft forks. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 31 (Fear), yet ETF inflows hit their best week since April, suggesting holders are moving custody preferences rather than exiting.

**The catch.** The minority chain is economically marginal right now, but the underlying debate over inscription data on Bitcoin is unresolved and will resurface. Any exchange or custodian that has not explicitly mapped its UTXO exposure may find edge cases as replay protection details emerge.
