<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. **What happened.** Senate Republicans circulated a 635-page revised CLARITY Act draft, framing it as a take-it-or-leave-it offer to Democrats ahead of a procedural vote on Tuesday. Trump accepted ethics language that bars senior officials and their spouses from crypto-related dealings, and separately agreed to let state attorneys general enforce those provisions, which was a significant sticking point for the minority.

**Why it matters.** Republicans say the revised text absorbs roughly 120 Democratic demands, making a cloture vote plausible for the first time. Polymarket now prices passage at 30%, up from single digits earlier this week, though that still implies a likely failure, and the Fear and Greed Index sitting at 57 after a 14-point slide suggests the market is not pricing in a clean win either.

**The catch.** Democrats have not publicly committed any votes, and the bill's sheer length gives dissenters plenty of surface area to object. The ethics carve-outs Trump accepted are also narrower than what progressive critics wanted, so the whip count remains genuinely uncertain going into the weekend.
