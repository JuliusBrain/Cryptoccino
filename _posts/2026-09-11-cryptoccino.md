## Bitcoin logs a fourth straight down day, pinned below $80K as surging yields and oil squeeze risk appetite ahead of CPI.
{: #bitcoin-logs-a-fourth-straight-down-day-pinned-below-80k-as-surging-yields-and-oil-squeeze-risk-appetite-ahead-of-cpi}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/bitcoin-slips-further-below-80-000-as-the-range-trade-resumes) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/10/bitcoin-trades-near-usd78-000-as-memecoins-small-caps-lead-a-broad-crypto-retreat) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/10/live-updates-bitcoin-etfs-post-a-second-straight-outflow-while-every-other-fund-turns-green) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/377903/bitcoin-price-golden-cross-coming)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">56<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−18 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin hovered near $78,000, extending a four-session losing run as climbing Treasury yields and oil prices drained appetite for risk across the board. Memecoins and small-caps led the drawdown, with Dogecoin off roughly 5%, while Bitcoin ETFs posted a second consecutive day of net outflows.

**Why it matters.** The move is macro-driven rather than crypto-specific: 30-year mortgage rates hit a 14-month high, diesel prices have spiked sharply, and the August CPI print due imminently could shift Fed expectations further. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 56, still nominally in Greed territory, but has shed 18 points over the past week, a signal that the confidence holding this range is eroding fast.

**The catch.** Bulls have a technical argument: a golden cross on Bitcoin's chart is forming for the first time since November, and Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong publicly called a cycle bottom, targeting $400K by 2030. That kind of exec-level optimism can read as a sentiment indicator in itself, particularly when it arrives mid-drawdown.
