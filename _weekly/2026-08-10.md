    <h1>Weekly Recap — 4 Aug–10 Aug 2026</h1>
  </header>
<p class="weekly-intro">The week had two parallel storylines that rarely intersected. On the legislative front, the Clarity Act crept toward a September procedural vote after Senate Democrats blocked pre-recess action, leaving market structure uncertainty intact and XRP as the most visible casualty. Meanwhile, a rolling hardware-wallet security crisis — the Coldcard exploit — forced a concrete reckoning with self-custody risk, visibly redirecting capital into ETF wrappers and producing the best weekly ETF inflows since April. Underneath both threads, institutional infrastructure kept advancing regardless: BlackRock tokenised $311 billion in European money market assets on public Ethereum, Mastercard closed the BVNK deal, and Wintermute secured broker-dealer registration. The week ended with markets flat in price but structurally busier than the charts suggested.</p>
