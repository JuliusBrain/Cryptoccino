## SEC Bypasses Congress With Tokenised-Stock Exemption, Opening Onchain Equity Trading to Compliant Venues.
{: #sec-bypasses-congress-with-tokenised-stock-exemption-opening-onchain-equity-trading-to-compliant-venues}

[`sec_press`](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-17-sec-releases-innovation-exemption-415324) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/09/17/sec-rolls-out-long-awaited-innovation-exemption-for-tokenized-securities-venues) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/sec-issues-guidance-to-tokenize-stocks-after-clarity-act-failure) [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sec-temporary-exemption-tokenized-us-stock-trading) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378492/sec-innovation-exemption-tokenized-stocks-clarity-act)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">56<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Hours after the Clarity Act failed its Senate cloture vote, the SEC released its long-prepared Innovation Exemption, granting qualifying Tokenised Securities Venues temporary relief from exchange-registration requirements so they can trade tokenised NMS stocks on public blockchains. The order comes with trading caps, transparency obligations, and a request for public comment, positioning it explicitly as a bridge measure while Congress stalls. OG.com, the Crypto.com sister exchange, was immediately named as having received SEC clearance for single-stock perpetual futures under the same regulatory wave.

**Why it matters.** This is the most structurally significant US regulatory move for crypto-TradFi convergence since spot ETF approval: real US equity exposure, settled onchain, with a credible legal footing. Robinhood Chain already reported $10 billion in stock-token volume, up 672%, before the exemption even landed, signalling latent demand. The SEC is deliberately using executive-branch rulemaking to advance what Congress could not, a playbook that may define the next cycle.

**The catch.** The exemption is temporary and conditional, meaning venues operate under ongoing SEC oversight with caps that constrain scale, and companies retain the right to block tokenisation of their own shares. Synthetics tracking stock prices are explicitly excluded, limiting DeFi-native use cases. The Clarity Act's failure leaves the broader legal architecture unresolved, and any administration change could rescind the exemption before it matures into settled precedent.
