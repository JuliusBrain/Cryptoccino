## Hot September CPI revives Fed hike odds, triggering nearly $1B in crypto liquidations.
{: #hot-september-cpi-revives-fed-hike-odds-triggering-nearly-1b-in-crypto-liquidations}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/15/nearly-usd1-billion-in-crypto-bets-liquidated-as-bitcoin-ether-and-xrp-slide) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/14/u-s-september-cpi-comes-in-hot-sending-bitcoin-sharply-lower) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-14-bitcoin-falls-below-82000-core-inflation-fed-rate-hike-418582) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380950/bitcoin-falls-hot-inflation-fed-hike-odds) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-falls-below-81k-liquidations/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/876m-long-positions-liquidated-crypto-24-hours/)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">53<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−11 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** September CPI printed at 3.7%, above consensus, pushing Treasury yields sharply higher and sending bitcoin from above $83,000 to below $81,000 in hours. The move cascaded through derivatives: close to $1B in leveraged longs were liquidated across BTC, ETH, and XRP. Asian equity markets followed, with South Korea's Kospi dropping 8% and AI chip stocks in Japan and Korea tumbling double digits.

**Why it matters.** A rate hike is now back on the table as a live scenario, not a tail risk, which reframes the macro backdrop that crypto bulls have been leaning on since early 2026. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 53 — neutral — but has shed 11 points in seven days, suggesting sentiment was already softening before this catalyst arrived. Bitcoin mining stocks fell more than 10% on the same session, compounding pain for operators carrying AI-infrastructure debt.

**The catch.** The liquidation flush removes the most fragile leverage from the system, which can set up cleaner price structure, but only if the macro overhang resolves. With no Fed meeting imminent and CPI now printing above target for consecutive months, the window for a near-term recovery narrative is narrowing. Watch whether spot ETF flows — BlackRock's crypto ETF assets are 63% above their 2025 trough but still well below peak — hold or reverse in the next 48 hours.
