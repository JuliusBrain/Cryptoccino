## Binance Reopens Withdrawals After $6.3B Hack. Users Pull $3.6B in the First Day.
{: #binance-reopens-withdrawals-after-63b-hack-users-pull-36b-in-the-first-day}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-01-binance-sees-3-64-billion-in-net-outflows-after-reopening-withdrawals-following-record-hack-432259) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/01/binance-opens-crypto-withdrawals-in-phases-starting-with-bitcoin-following-usd6-3-billion-hack) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/binance-3-8-billion-withdrawals-after-hack/) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/02/bitcoin-claws-back-toward-usd79-000-as-markets-shake-off-crypto-s-biggest-hack)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">37<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−13 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Binance restored withdrawals in phases following the record $6.3B hack, prioritising bitcoin first. Users moved roughly $3.6 billion out of the exchange within the first ten hours, representing about 2.1% of its stated $171 billion in assets. Volumes have since tapered off, and bitcoin is clawing back toward $79,000.

**Why it matters.** The scale of the withdrawal run is the clearest signal yet of how fragile exchange trust becomes the moment reserves are in question, even when the exchange holds. Spot bitcoin ETFs shed $643 million on Friday, their largest single-day outflow in four months, closing out October with roughly $516 million in net outflows. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 37 and down 13 points over the week suggests this is not a market shaking it off with confidence.

**The catch.** Binance's own figures show it remains solvent on the numbers, but the phased reopening and the sheer velocity of outflows will feed solvency-doubt cycles regardless. The $6.3B figure also still makes this the largest single exchange hack on record by a wide margin, and attribution to North Korean actors means recovery of stolen funds is effectively zero. How CZ and team communicate reserves over the coming days will matter as much as the balance sheet itself.
