<p class="weekly-intro">This was a week where structural plumbing advanced quietly while louder events — a stalled bill, a record hack, macro turbulence — drew most of the attention. The CLARITY Act's failure defined the political mood, but the more durable story was institutional infrastructure deepening in several directions at once: Goldman connecting a flagship fund to crypto rails, Coinbase completing vertical integration, Lloyds and Visa settling in USDC. The Bitget hack, attributed to North Korea with over a billion dollars stolen in 2026 so far, was the sharpest reminder that the same week's progress in custody and settlement is not yet matched by security across the ecosystem.</p>
