Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">48<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−7 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slipped back below $80,000 over the weekend and is struggling to reclaim that level ahead of a dense macro schedule: FOMC, ECB and BOJ decisions, Coinbase earnings, and US midterm results all land this week. AI-related equity selling pulled risk appetite broadly lower, with Bitcoin ETFs eking out $110 million in net inflows while Ether funds bled $88.6 million.

**Why it matters.** The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 48 (Neutral, down 7 on the week) tells you the crowd is neither panicking nor buying conviction — it is waiting. HashKey's Tim Sun flagged $75,000 as the next meaningful support if the Fed signals a prolonged hold, and a $68 million options position is positioned for a breakout to $90,000 on a benign midterm outcome.

**Watch.** Galaxy Research published a note declaring the bear market over since June, pointing to a reclaimed 50-week moving average and fading long-term holder selling. That thesis gets tested in real time this week: a hawkish surprise from Powell or a contested midterm result would stress it quickly. Strategy notably paused bitcoin purchases and spent $205 million buying back STRC preferred shares instead, a signal that even the most committed treasury buyer sees better value elsewhere right now.
