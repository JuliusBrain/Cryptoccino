## Bitcoin Clears $81K as Fed Pause Bets Surge and Short Sellers Absorb $415 Million in Losses.
{: #bitcoin-clears-81k-as-fed-pause-bets-surge-and-short-sellers-absorb-415-million-in-losses}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">74<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Fed Governor Christopher Waller signalled he could support holding rates steady, dollar weakness accelerated, and Bitcoin punched through $81,000 for the first time in weeks. The broader crypto market cap added roughly $135 billion in 24 hours, with Zcash leading altcoins at plus-15% and PONS extending its Robinhood Chain-linked run. Around $415 million in short positions were liquidated across the session, with a particularly sharp $58 million squeeze in a single hour.

**Why it matters.** The move is macro-driven rather than crypto-native: yen strength, fading hike odds, and the dollar's worst hedge positioning since 2015 are all pulling in the same direction. Bitcoin's correlation with gold has hit a six-year high, and IBIT pulled in $300 million in a single day, suggesting institutional rotation rather than retail FOMO. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 74, deep in Greed territory, which makes the macro tailwind the more credible engine here than sentiment alone.

**The catch.** Glassnode is sceptical of the equity decoupling narrative, noting that similar divergences have reversed quickly in prior cycles. US ETF demand remains choppy, and Ethereum and XRP ETFs both snapped multi-week inflow streaks on Wednesday. A rate-pause signal is not a rate cut, and any hawkish pivot from the Fed could unwind this move rapidly.
