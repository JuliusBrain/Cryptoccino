## Treasury Yields Hit a 16-Year High and Bitcoin Pays the Price
{: #treasury-yields-hit-a-16-year-high-and-bitcoin-pays-the-price}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/24/dogecoin-down-8-bitcoin-under-usd84-000-as-treasury-yields-hit-highest-level-since-2007) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/23/live-updates-bitcoin-slips-under-usd86-000-as-money-rotates-into-bch-and-zec) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/global-bond-rout-deepens-with-oil-prices-above-100-ft/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+21 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US Treasury yields pushed to their highest level since 2007 as a global bond rout deepened alongside oil prices above $100 a barrel. Bitcoin fell back through $84,000 and Dogecoin shed 8%, reversing a run that had taken BTC to seven-month highs earlier in the week.

**Why it matters.** Rising yields tighten the risk-asset discount rate and strengthen the dollar, two direct headwinds for crypto. The timing is awkward: the Fear and Greed Index sits at 71 and has climbed 21 points in seven days, meaning the market was leaning heavily long just as the macro backdrop turned.

**The catch.** The selloff arrived on the same day as bitcoin's $16 billion quarterly options expiry, a call-heavy book that analysts had flagged as supportive above $87K-88K resistance. That pin level now looks distant, and the macro pressure may persist if oil and yields stay elevated through the weekend.
