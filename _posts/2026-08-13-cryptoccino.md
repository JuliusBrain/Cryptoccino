## Harmony confirms unauthorised minting of 4 billion ONE tokens, chain weighs rollback as price craters 37%.
{: #harmony-confirms-unauthorised-minting-of-4-billion-one-tokens-chain-weighs-rollback-as-price-craters-37}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-08-12-harmony-confirms-exploit-one-token-411527) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/375390/harmonys-one-sinks-37-after-attacker-mints-4-billion-tokens) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/12/bitcoin-holds-near-usd64-000-as-u-s-inflation-data-looms-harmony-exploit-rattles-altcoins)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** An attacker minted four billion ONE tokens without authorisation on Harmony, confirmed by the team after on-chain researcher Juiceberg flagged the anomaly. ONE dropped 37% on the news, rattling altcoin sentiment across the board.

**Why it matters.** Unbacked minting is a protocol-level failure, not a bridge or UI mishap. The Fear and Greed Index was already sitting at 29 before this landed, and the incident adds a fresh supply shock to a market that has little appetite for bad news right now.

**The catch.** The team is publicly weighing a chain rollback, which would reverse the exploit but also wipe every legitimate transaction executed since. That choice is a reputational cliff edge: rollbacks signal the chain is not credibly immutable, and Harmony has been here before after the 2022 Horizon bridge hack.
