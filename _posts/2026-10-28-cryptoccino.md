## Datavault AI loses half its market cap after disclosing it cannot access the bitcoin backing a $150 million deal.
{: #datavault-ai-loses-half-its-market-cap-after-disclosing-it-cannot-access-the-bitcoin-backing-a-150-million-deal}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/28/bitcoin-treasury-datavault-ai-plunges-50-after-firm-says-it-can-t-access-its-btc) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-27-datavault-ai-stock-plummets-bitcoin-deal-431962) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/datavault-ai-shares-crash-biconomy-bitcoin/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">51<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−12 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Datavault AI disclosed that it cannot withdraw bitcoin held on Biconomy, the custodian tied to its $150 million share-purchase deal with Scilex. The stock fell roughly 50% intraday on the news, one of the sharper single-day collapses in the bitcoin-treasury cohort.

**Why it matters.** The episode is a direct challenge to the treasury-strategy playbook: adding BTC to the balance sheet only works if you can actually move the coins. Custody risk, rather than price risk, is now the operational question boards need to answer before they replicate the MicroStrategy trade.

**The catch.** Fear and Greed sits at 51, neutral, down 12 points over the past week, so sentiment was already fragile before this landed. Datavault is small, but it hands ammunition to everyone arguing that corporate bitcoin treasuries are governance liabilities in disguise, and regulators will notice.
