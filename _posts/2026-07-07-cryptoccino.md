## Strategy Sells 3,588 BTC to Fund Dividends as Its Preferred Stack Trades Underwater.

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">27<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+12 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Strategy disposed of 3,588 BTC for roughly $216 million last week under what it is calling a BTC Monetisation Programme, using the proceeds to fund preferred stock dividends. The sale is the sharpest acceleration in the firm's bitcoin offloading since it began unwinding positions, and comes against a reported $8.3 billion quarterly loss.

**Why it matters.** Cantor flagged that the firm's STRC preferred shares are trading below par, and says restoring that parity is the precondition for any recovery in the equity. The forced-selling dynamic is precisely what critics of concentrated corporate BTC treasuries warned about: a coin-price decline triggers dilution or disposal, which can weigh further on the underlying asset. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 27, deep in Fear territory, though the seven-day delta of plus 12 suggests the worst of the sentiment rout may have passed.

**The catch.** Grayscale's take is that the sales are net positive, arguing they will restore financing confidence and help bitcoin carve a more durable floor. Bernstein is holding its $150,000 year-end target and characterises the current 54% drawdown as milder than prior cycles. Both readings depend on no further forced selling materialising from Strategy or imitators.
