    <h1>Weekly Recap — 15 Sep–21 Sep 2026</h1>
  </header>
<p class="weekly-intro">The week opened with a legislative rupture and closed with a market that largely shrugged it off. The Clarity Act's Senate failure stripped crypto of its most anticipated near-term catalyst, yet Bitcoin absorbed a simultaneous Federal Reserve rate hike — the first in three years — and held above $76,000, inverting the 2022 playbook. What replaced the legislative story was a structural one: regulators, exchanges, and TradFi institutions moved quickly to fill the vacuum, with the SEC's tokenised-stock exemption, the CFTC's preemptive rulemaking, and a string of institutional product launches signalling that the industry's buildout is no longer waiting on Congress.</p>
