## Bitcoin breaks $80,000 as Samsung-driven risk-off and a hawkish Fed tilt combine for the first time since the rally stalled.
{: #bitcoin-breaks-80000-as-samsung-driven-risk-off-and-a-hawkish-fed-tilt-combine-for-the-first-time-since-the-rally-stalled}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/29/bitcoin-dips-below-usd80-000-as-samsung-led-risk-aversion-in-tech-and-a-hawkish-fed-weigh-on-crypto) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382092/fed-hold-interest-rate-steady-bitcoin-december-rate-hike) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/28/fed-leaves-interest-rates-unchanged-as-expected) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/bitcoin-traders-brace-for-swings-on-11-billion-options-expiry)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">51<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−17 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slipped below $80,000 overnight, reversing a brief rebound above $81,000 that had followed SK Hynix's record quarter. Samsung's disappointing foundry numbers revived tech risk-off sentiment, and the Fed's 10-2 hold vote — with two members favouring an outright hike — kept the hawkish undertone alive heading into December.

**Why it matters.** The two dissents are the tell: market pricing for a December hike is no longer negligible, which puts dollar strength and tighter liquidity conditions back on the table just as $11 billion in Bitcoin options expire this week and midterm elections approach. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 51 — neutral — down 17 points in seven days, meaning the sentiment cushion that absorbed earlier volatility has largely deflated.

**The catch.** Galaxy Research data shows fast money — hedge funds and banks — already cut roughly 68,000 BTC of spot ETF exposure as the cash-and-carry trade dried up, with sovereign funds and wealth platforms absorbing the flow. If that rotation is incomplete, the next leg down could find thinner institutional support than the ETF narrative implies.
