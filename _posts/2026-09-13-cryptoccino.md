## Revolut Surrendered Passport Scans and Bitcoin Transaction Histories to a Spoofed Government Email
{: #revolut-surrendered-passport-scans-and-bitcoin-transaction-histories-to-a-spoofed-government-email}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-12-revolut-says-customer-kyc-bitcoin-transaction-data-exposed-after-fake-request-from-govt-domain-414516) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/09/12/bitcoin-activity-passports-exposed-after-revolut-falls-for-fake-government-request) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378114/revolut-passports-bitcoin-activity-data-breach) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/revolut-data-breach-fake-government-requests/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/revolut-phishing-attack-customer-data-exposed/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">61<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−12 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Revolut confirmed it fulfilled a fraudulent law-enforcement data request sent from a spoofed government agency email domain, exposing a limited number of customers' passport scans and full Bitcoin transaction histories. The attacker used a legitimate-looking domain to pass email authentication checks, bypassing the standard signals Revolut's compliance team would rely on to verify a request.

**Why it matters.** This is not a protocol exploit or a hot-wallet drain. It is a social-engineering attack that worked precisely because it mimicked the formal legal-intercept process fintechs are obligated to answer quickly. Onchain investigator ZachXBT flagged that the targeting pattern suggests high-net-worth users were the primary objective, which makes the Bitcoin transaction data the more dangerous payload.

**The catch.** Revolut has disclosed very little about how many users are affected or which jurisdiction the fake request purported to come from, and has not confirmed whether it has tightened its verification procedure since the incident. Any fintech holding KYC plus onchain activity records is a structurally attractive target for the same playbook, and the fix, out-of-band verification for every data request, is rarely enforced in practice.
