## Fed Week Puts Crypto on a Wire: Citadel Calls a Surprise Hike, BTC Slips, Options Hedges Come Off
{: #fed-week-puts-crypto-on-a-wire-citadel-calls-a-surprise-hike-btc-slips-options-hedges-come-off}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/bitcoin-weakens-in-asia-trading-as-fed-rate-decision-looms) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/citadel-securities-predicts-surprise-fed-rate-hike-this-week/) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/374453/prediction-market-traders-brace-for-surprise-fed-rate-hike) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/27/bitcoin-options-traders-are-dropping-their-hedges-going-into-the-fed-meeting) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/citadel-fed-rate-hike-crypto-impact/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Citadel Securities flagged a surprise rate hike as a live possibility at this week's FOMC meeting, sending prediction-market odds of a July hike to 27%, up double digits in 24 hours. Bitcoin softened in early Asia trading on Tuesday, retreating toward $65,000, while the dollar hit a one-month high. Bitcoin options traders simultaneously stripped out hedges ahead of the decision, a positioning move that leaves the market exposed to a sharp move in either direction.

**Why it matters.** A surprise hike would be the first in this cycle's pause and would reprice rate-sensitive assets across the board, crypto included. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 29 (Fear), yet positioning is turning complacent, a combination that tends to amplify disorderly moves when the surprise arrives. Bitcoin ETFs shed $465 million over two late-week sessions, led by BlackRock's IBIT, snapping a seven-day inflow streak, so the bid under spot is thinner than it looks.

**The catch.** The same week brings Coinbase and Strategy earnings, UK and Japan rate decisions, and continued US-Iran ceasefire optimism that already knocked oil down 7-11% and lifted risk assets Monday. If the Fed holds and tone stays dovish, the setup is a squeeze higher. The week has too many cross-currents to trade with conviction in either direction.
