## Bitcoin decouples downward as stocks rally and the death cross closes in.
{: #bitcoin-decouples-downward-as-stocks-rally-and-the-death-cross-closes-in}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-27/bitcoin-bounces-as-optimism-returns-to-markets-after-holiday) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/27/bitcoin-on-course-to-end-seven-week-losing-streak-while-trailing-stock-rebound) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/384177/bitcoin-4-scary-charts-crypto-winter-bear-market) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/daybook-us/2026/11/27/bitcoin-death-cross-is-coming-here-s-what-happened-after-previous-13)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">10<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin briefly reclaimed $61,000 on Thanksgiving before sliding back below $60,000 on Friday, even as the Nasdaq extended its post-holiday bounce. The asset is on track to snap a seven-week losing streak but only just, and it is visibly lagging a broad equity recovery.

**Why it matters.** The failure to participate in a risk-on session is a bearish signal in its own right, suggesting crypto-specific selling pressure rather than macro headwinds alone. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 10, deep in extreme fear, and yet that distress is not attracting the dip-buyers who typically show up at these levels, which makes the divergence harder to dismiss.

**The catch.** A death cross on Bitcoin's daily chart is imminent. Historical precedent across the previous 13 occurrences is mixed, so it is not a clean directional signal, but it will give momentum traders a fresh narrative to sell into. Combined with $7 billion in options expiry this week and hedges being placed against a move to $40,000, the near-term setup favours continued chop at best.
