## Bitcoin's best quarter since 2024 ends on a hesitant note as PCE relief meets a stubborn yield ceiling.
{: #bitcoins-best-quarter-since-2024-ends-on-a-hesitant-note-as-pce-relief-meets-a-stubborn-yield-ceiling}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/01/bitcoin-s-soft-inflation-pop-to-usd85-500-fades-as-bond-yields-refuse-to-fall) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-30-bitcoin-pce-inflation-october-rate-hike-417335) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379705/bitcoin-jumps-cool-pce-inflation-data) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/30/live-updates-bitcoin-below-usd84-000-ahead-of-pce-inflation-data-micron-earnings)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">74<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+3 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** August core PCE came in at 0.2%, softer than feared, briefly pushing Bitcoin to $85,500 and knocking October rate-hike bets lower. The move faded quickly as Treasury yields stayed elevated near 20-year highs, capping any macro-driven continuation. BTC settled back near $83,700 to close out the quarter.

**Why it matters.** Q3 still lands as Bitcoin's best quarterly performance since 2024, and Ether's best since 2021, so the structural trend holds even if the daily print disappointed. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 74 (Greed) with only a three-point gain over seven days, a reminder that sentiment is running ahead of the macro permission structure needed to sustain a leg higher.

**The catch.** Rising Treasury yields are functioning as a competing risk-off signal, eroding the reflexive crypto-as-hedge narrative. Spot Bitcoin ETFs did extend their inflow streak to nine consecutive days, pulling in $66 million Tuesday, but Fidelity's FBTC saw $125 million in client redemptions on the same day, suggesting rotation within the ETF complex rather than clean institutional accumulation.
