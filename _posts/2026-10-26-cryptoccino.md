## Bitcoin retreats below $80,000 as traders cut risk ahead of the Fed.
{: #bitcoin-retreats-below-80000-as-traders-cut-risk-ahead-of-the-fed}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/26/bitcoin-slips-under-usd80-000-as-traders-de-risk-ahead-of-fed-meeting) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/117m-crypto-long-positions-liquidated-24-hours/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/117m-in-crypto-long-positions-liquidated-amid-market-downturn/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/spot-bitcoin-etfs-443m-net-outflows/) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381777/bitcoin-etfs-snap-two-week-losing-streak-despite-late-week-slide)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">50<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−9 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin dropped below $80,000 as traders pared exposure ahead of the Federal Reserve's upcoming meeting. The move triggered roughly $117 million in long liquidations over 24 hours, with spot Bitcoin ETFs recording $443 million in net outflows on the day, keeping October firmly in the red despite a weekly inflow of around $301 million that snapped a two-week losing streak.

**Why it matters.** The $80k level has acted as a psychological and technical floor for several weeks; a sustained breach shifts near-term positioning meaningfully. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 50, neutral and down 9 points on the week, suggesting the market came into this move without the froth that typically cushions a flush, which makes the level harder to defend on sentiment alone.

**The catch.** Fed meetings have a history of producing sharper recoveries than the pre-meeting selloff implies, particularly when positioning is already light. The weekly ETF inflow figure shows institutional appetite has not disappeared, just paused, so any dovish signal could reverse the move quickly. Watch whether spot demand re-emerges at $78k to $79k before the decision.
