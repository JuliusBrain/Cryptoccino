## Bitcoin's sharpest liquidation flush since 2022 clears $1.8 billion before price snaps back above $60,000.
{: #bitcoins-sharpest-liquidation-flush-since-2022-clears-18-billion-before-price-snaps-back-above-60000}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/21/bitcoin-bounces-9-from-two-year-low-to-retake-usd60-000-as-traders-eye-monday-s-stock-open) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-longest-weekly-losing-streak-since-2022/) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-21/crypto-crashes-pile-up-as-once-diehard-fans-chase-new-bets) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383754/ai-bubble-finally-popping-nvidia-crashes-48-wall-street-alarm)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">11<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slid to its lowest level in two years before bouncing roughly 9% to reclaim $60,000, as a cascade of leveraged long positions unwound into a single liquidation wave worth $1.8 billion across the market. The move extends what is shaping up as Bitcoin's longest consecutive weekly losing streak since 2022, with capital visibly rotating toward AI-linked equities.

**Why it matters.** The flush is both a positioning reset and a macro signal. Nvidia's 48% drawdown and broader tech stress are pulling risk appetite away from crypto, and the Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 11, deep in Extreme Fear, with almost no recovery in a week, suggests this is not a dip-buying moment for the casual holder. Operators running levered books or treasury positions need to treat current volatility as structural, not episodic.

**The catch.** BTCS, an Ether treasury firm, has already filed a warning with the SEC that its $54 million Aave-collateralised loan is approaching liquidation thresholds. If ETH continues to slide alongside BTC, on-chain forced selling could add another leg down before the stock open gives any directional cue.
