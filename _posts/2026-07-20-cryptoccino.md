## US-Iran Conflict Pushes Brent Above $90 and Rattles Risk Assets Across the Board.
{: #us-iran-conflict-pushes-brent-above-90-and-rattles-risk-assets-across-the-board}

[`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/brent-crude-90-us-iran-crypto-impact/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/iran-closes-strait-of-hormuz-after-us-airstrikes-oil-prices-surge/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/irgc-claims-us-radar-destruction-kuwait-bitcoin-volatile/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/irgc-strait-hormuz-oil-tankers-crypto-impact/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Now ten days into active US-Iran exchanges, Brent crude crossed $90 and the IRGC claims strikes on US facilities in Kuwait, Jordan, and Iraq, as well as disruption to oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. CENTCOM disputed some of those claims, but the ambiguity alone has been enough to move energy and equity markets hard. Bitcoin slid toward $99.5K on the news, giving up the psychological six-figure level it had been holding.

**Why it matters.** A sustained Hormuz disruption would take roughly 20% of seaborne oil off the market, feeding directly into the inflation expectations that have already been pressuring rate-cut timelines. For crypto, that means the macro tailwind from rate optimism gets weaker precisely when the Fear &amp; Greed Index is already sitting at 29, deep in Fear territory, suggesting the market has limited cushion for additional shocks. Taiwan's TAIEX simultaneously posted its largest-ever single-day point drop as retail investors unwound leveraged positions, adding another leg to the broad risk-off move.

**The catch.** Several of the most dramatic IRGC claims, including tanker strikes and radar facility destruction, remain unverified or actively disputed by US defence officials. Markets are pricing a worst-case scenario that may be partly Iranian information operations. China's PBoC injecting 398.5 billion yuan via reverse repos and deploying $7.38 billion in state fund purchases to arrest its own equity slide suggests Beijing is already in defensive mode, which limits one potential source of global stabilisation.
