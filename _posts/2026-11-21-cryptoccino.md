## Bitcoin Drops Below $57,000 for the First Time in Over Two Years as the AI Trade Unwinds Violently
{: #bitcoin-drops-below-57000-for-the-first-time-in-over-two-years-as-the-ai-trade-unwinds-violently}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/21/bitcoin-tanks-below-usd57-000-for-the-first-time-in-two-years-and-its-not-alone) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/bitcoin-falls-to-more-than-two-year-low-as-global-markets-sink) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383702/bitcoin-breaks-below-60k-crypto-fear-greed-index-lowest) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-falls-lowest-level-since-september-2024/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">12<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin broke below $57,000 on Friday, extending a three-day slide that has now taken it to its lowest print since late 2024. The move accelerated after Anthropic's Nasdaq debut disappointed, Nvidia shed 23% on the week, and broader equity volatility gauges spiked across the board.

**Why it matters.** This is a regime shift, not a dip. Spot bitcoin ETFs bled $582 million in a single session, with BlackRock's IBIT responsible for nearly all of it, pushing November's total outflows close to $3.5 billion. The Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 12, deep in Extreme Fear, yet institutional outflows are still accelerating — the usual contrarian signal is not holding.

**The catch.** JPMorgan puts bitcoin's estimated production cost at $86,000, meaning spot is now trading at a roughly $29,000 discount to that benchmark, a spread that historically precedes either a sharp capitulation or a supply-side shakeout among miners. One distressed miner has already sold 267 BTC to service debt and is still signalling it may not survive. Watch the hashrate trend over the next two weeks.
