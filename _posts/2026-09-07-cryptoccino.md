## Liquid Network Halted After Purported White-Hats Drain $320 Million in Bitcoin From the Sidechain
{: #liquid-network-halted-after-purported-white-hats-drain-320-million-in-bitcoin-from-the-sidechain}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-09-06-liquid-network-pauses-after-purported-white-hat-hackers-withdraw-320-million-in-bitcoin-413626) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/07/bitcoin-network-used-by-exchanges-hit-by-usd320-million-exploit-hackers-claim-they-re-the-good-guys) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/bitcoin-network-says-320-million-stolen-in-latest-crypto-hack)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+9 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Blockstream's Liquid Network, a Bitcoin federation sidechain used by exchanges for faster BTC settlement, has been paused after unknown actors withdrew approximately $320 million in LBTC. The parties behind the withdrawal claim they acted as white-hats exploiting a vulnerability before a malicious actor could. Exchanges have suspended LBTC deposits and withdrawals while Blockstream attempts to make contact with those responsible.

**Why it matters.** Liquid sits beneath a meaningful slice of exchange settlement infrastructure, so a prolonged pause creates real operational friction for desks relying on it for interexchange BTC moves. The self-declared white-hat framing does not reduce the immediate damage: funds are out of the federation, the network is halted, and exchange customers are locked out of LBTC flows. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 71 and has risen nine points in a week, meaning this hits at a moment of elevated risk appetite rather than defensive positioning.

**The catch.** White-hat claims without prior disclosure or a coordinated responsible-disclosure process are difficult to verify and create serious legal exposure for whoever holds the funds. Blockstream has not confirmed the vulnerability exists, nor offered a timeline for resuming the network. Until funds are returned and an independent post-mortem published, exchanges should treat LBTC settlement as unavailable.
