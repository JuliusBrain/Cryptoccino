## China GDP beat lifts Bitcoin and risk assets as ETF outflows and weakening on-chain metrics complicate the relief rally.
{: #china-gdp-beat-lifts-bitcoin-and-risk-assets-as-etf-outflows-and-weakening-on-chain-metrics-complicate-the-relief-rally}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/19/bitcoin-korea-s-kospi-and-nasdaq-futures-rally-as-china-gdp-beats-estimates) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/18/wall-street-freaked-out-friday-vix-and-bitcoin-say-it-may-not-last) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381246/bitcoin-etfs-shed-nearly-1-billion-two-weeks-uptober-red) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-mvrv-below-30th-percentile-87k/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">59<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−10 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** China's Q3 GDP came in ahead of expectations, triggering broad risk-on moves across Nasdaq futures, Korea's Kospi, and Bitcoin. The move follows a Friday sell-off on Wall Street that VIX positioning and BTC options flow had already flagged as likely short-lived. Analysts tracking the BTC options market noted the fear looked overdone heading into the weekend.

**Why it matters.** The macro catalyst gives bulls a narrative, but the underlying flows are messier. Spot Bitcoin ETFs shed roughly $910 million over the past two weeks, October's seasonal tailwind nowhere in sight. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 59 (Greed) but has slid 10 points in seven days, a setup where sentiment and price can diverge quickly if the macro bid fades.

**The catch.** On-chain signals are not supportive. Bitcoin's MVRV ratio dropped below the 30th percentile after a rejection at $87k, and mid-term holders have cut their exchange transfer share from 40.4% in June to just 4.4% now, suggesting reduced selling pressure but equally thin fresh demand. Year-end $100k calls remain contested among analysts, with inflation data still the swing factor.
