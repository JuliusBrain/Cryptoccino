## Richard Heart Arrested in Turkey as Police Seize $7M Cash, Six Cold Wallets, and an Arsenal
{: #richard-heart-arrested-in-turkey-as-police-seize-7m-cash-six-cold-wallets-and-an-arsenal}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-10-17-turkey-confirms-hex-founder-richard-hearts-arrest-says-police-seized-7-15-million-and-six-cold-wallets-418852)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">53<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−8 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Turkish authorities have confirmed the arrest of Hex founder Richard Heart, with police raiding 15 addresses and walking away with roughly $7.15 million in cash, six cold wallets, seven firearms, and 1,400 rounds of ammunition. The scale of the operation suggests a coordinated, intelligence-led effort rather than an opportunistic sweep.

**Why it matters.** Heart has spent years dodging a US SEC fraud and unregistered securities suit filed in 2023, and Turkey's move makes extradition or further international cooperation a live question. The cold wallet seizure is the more consequential detail for anyone watching asset recovery, since it raises the question of whether authorities can access those funds or whether they sit permanently locked.

**The catch.** Turkish charges and the nature of the underlying allegations have not been fully disclosed, so it remains unclear whether this is linked to the SEC action, local financial crime statutes, or something else entirely. How cooperative Heart's legal team is with wallet access will determine whether any restitution to Hex investors is ever realistic.
