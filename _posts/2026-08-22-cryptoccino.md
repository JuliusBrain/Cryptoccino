## Bitcoin posts its strongest week in two years, shorts get torched for $1.2 billion, but $80K is proving harder than it looks.
{: #bitcoin-posts-its-strongest-week-in-two-years-shorts-get-torched-for-12-billion-but-80k-is-proving-harder-than-it-looks}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/21/bitcoin-tops-usd77-000-as-best-week-since-2023-pulls-altcoins-along-for-the-ride) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/21/bitcoin-ether-and-solana-climb-as-another-usd1-billion-shorts-get-wiped-out) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/376183/bitcoin-price-higher-1-2-billion-shorts-liquidated) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/376244/wall-street-washington-bitcoin-rally) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/21/treasury-s-latest-measure-isn-t-qe-or-ycc-still-bitcoin-is-skyrocketing-here-s-why) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/daybook-us/2026/08/21/bitcoin-faces-usd80-000-test-as-thinner-weekend-liquidity-looms)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+37 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin surged 7.9% in a single session to touch $80,000 overnight before slipping back to the $77,000 range, recording its best weekly performance since late 2023. The move liquidated roughly $1.2 billion in short positions across the market, dragging ETH, SOL, and a broad basket of altcoins sharply higher. US spot Bitcoin ETFs pulled in $606 million on Thursday alone, with BlackRock accounting for 83% of the day's inflows.

**Why it matters.** The rally is not a single-factor story: institutional ETF demand, a softer dollar, Treasury bond buybacks suppressing yields, and a materially friendlier regulatory tone out of Washington are all compounding simultaneously. That confluence is more durable than a pure short-squeeze narrative, though the squeeze itself amplified the move considerably. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 71 and has climbed 37 points in seven days, a reminder that sentiment has repriced very fast from a standing start.

**The catch.** Bitcoin failed to hold $80,000 on the first attempt and now faces a weekend with thinner order books, which cuts both ways. Analysts are openly split on whether this is the opening leg of a sustained regime change or an overextended bounce in a still-uncertain macro environment. Fresh 50% US-Canada tariffs confirmed this morning add a macro wildcard that institutional desks will need to price into the week ahead.
