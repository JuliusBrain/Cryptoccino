## Bitcoin prints a 12-week low, sixth consecutive weekly loss, as macro pressure and $1.6 billion in liquidations erode the summer rally.
{: #bitcoin-prints-a-12-week-low-sixth-consecutive-weekly-loss-as-macro-pressure-and-16-billion-in-liquidations-erode-the-summer-rally}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/13/bitcoin-hits-12-week-low-as-dollar-rallies-on-interest-rate-outlook-liquidations-top-usd1-6-billion) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/13/bitcoin-heads-for-sixth-weekly-loss-as-anthropic-s-blockbuster-ipo-looms) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383231/bitcoin-hits-12-week-low-fed-rate-fears-undo-summer-rally) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-13-bitcoin-tumbles-below-69000-439643) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/bitcoin-s-billion-dollar-wipeouts-are-becoming-a-familiar-sight)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">13<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−12 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slid to $68,000, its lowest print in twelve weeks, as a strengthening dollar, rising rate-hike expectations, and fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation at Nvidia combined to flush levered longs. Total liquidations across crypto exceeded $1.6 billion in a single session, with long positions accounting for the overwhelming majority.

**Why it matters.** This is now the sixth straight weekly loss, and the move has unwound most of the late-summer rally. The Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 13, deep in Extreme Fear and down 12 points over seven days, yet spot buyers have not stepped in at scale. Bitcoin ETFs are now net negative on the year by over $1.5 billion, flipping a headline that bulls leaned on heavily through 2025.

**The catch.** Coinbase Institutional pinned the primary driver on shifting Fed rate expectations rather than any crypto-specific event, including the Binance hack. That macro read matters because it limits what any on-chain catalyst can fix near term. Momentum indicators are rolling over, and several desks are flagging a potential revisit of sub-$60,000 if the dollar continues to strengthen into year-end.
