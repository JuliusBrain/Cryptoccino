## AI Trade Implosion Drags Bitcoin to Seven-Week Low, Wipes $1 Billion in Longs.
{: #ai-trade-implosion-drags-bitcoin-to-seven-week-low-wipes-1-billion-in-longs}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-05-bitcoin-erases-post-election-gains-wall-street-selloff-liquidations-435434) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/bitcoin-sinks-to-seven-week-low-as-risk-off-mood-deepens) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/05/bitcoin-crypto-stocks-caught-in-ai-trade-meltdown-but-wintermute-trader-sees-glass-half-full) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382612/bitcoin-erases-election-rally-ai-stocks-plunge-428-million-crypto-longs)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">28<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin fell roughly 6% to a low of $75,700 on Thursday as a Nasdaq-led sell-off, centred on AI-exposed names, spilled hard into crypto. Ether dropped 7%, the S&amp;P 500 shed 3.8%, and cascading forced liquidations erased over $1 billion in long positions, undoing the entire post-election short-covering rally from Wednesday in a single session.

**Why it matters.** The correlation between high-beta AI equities and crypto continues to tighten at exactly the wrong moments, meaning macro risk-off events now transmit faster and harder into digital assets than the decoupling narrative allows. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 28 — deep fear, down two points on the week — suggests the market had already been fragile before Thursday's catalyst showed up. VanEck's note that Bitcoin miners are oversold after a 35% October rout adds context: this isn't a one-day wobble built on leverage excess, it is the continuation of a prolonged de-risking cycle.

**The catch.** Ark Invest stepped in to buy $2.44 million of Coinbase into the dip, and a Wintermute trader was publicly framing the glass as half-full, but neither data point changes the structural picture. Leverage is thin, demand remains tepid, and BlackRock's IBIT saw $104.97 million in net outflows on the same day. Until spot bid returns with conviction, any bounce is a relief rally into an overhang.
