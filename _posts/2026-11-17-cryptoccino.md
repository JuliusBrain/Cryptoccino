## Bitcoin's worst liquidation weekend since July leaves $60,000 as the last line traders are watching.
{: #bitcoins-worst-liquidation-weekend-since-july-leaves-60000-as-the-last-line-traders-are-watching}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/16/bitcoin-slips-to-usd64-000-after-usd2-billion-weekend-flush) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383366/bitcoin-2-billion-liquidation-weekend-traders-watching-60000) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/bitcoin-btc-options-traders-brace-slide-60-000-selloff) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/16/bitcoin-risks-falling-below-usd60-000-as-nvidia-earnings-threaten-to-deepen-crypto-sell-off)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">12<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−20 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin shed roughly $5,000 over the weekend, triggering over $2 billion in liquidations and touching its lowest print since mid-July before stabilising near $64,000-$65,000. The Nasdaq closed the week down 2.5%, dragging crypto with it, and ETF outflows compounded the spot pressure. Options positioning now clusters heavily around the $60,000 strike, which has become the consensus line in the sand.

**Why it matters.** This is Bitcoin's sixth consecutive weekly loss, a streak that has pushed the Fear &amp; Greed Index to 12 (Extreme Fear, down 20 points over seven days), yet the dominant narrative six months ago was that BTC decouples from tech during AI-driven risk-off. It has not. The correlation to Nasdaq remains tight, and with Nvidia earnings still pending, the macro overhang is not resolved.

**The catch.** Stablecoins are not absorbing the rotation: Bloomberg reports the stablecoin float is shrinking at its fastest rate since the Terra-Luna collapse in 2022, suggesting capital is leaving the ecosystem rather than waiting on the sideline. Strategy bought another 485 BTC for $33 million and Twenty One Capital added to its stack, but dip-buyers are thin relative to the size of the flush. Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan flagged further near-term downside as likely before any recovery takes hold.
