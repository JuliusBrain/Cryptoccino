## Bitcoin's correlation with gold hits a six-year peak as its link to the NASDAQ quietly unravels.
{: #bitcoins-correlation-with-gold-hits-a-six-year-peak-as-its-link-to-the-nasdaq-quietly-unravels}

[`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-gold-correlation-hits-six-year-high-as-nasdaq-link-weakens/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">73<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin's rolling correlation with gold has reached its highest level in six years, while its historically tight relationship with the NASDAQ has materially softened. The shift is showing up across multiple timeframes, not just a short-term blip driven by a single day's move.

**Why it matters.** If the rotation from risk-asset proxy to store-of-value proxy is sticky, it reshapes how macro funds and multi-asset allocators model BTC exposure. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 73 and barely moving on a week of geopolitical escalation is a mild data point in the same direction: the market is less reactive to equity-style sentiment than it used to be.

**The catch.** Correlations shift fast and often mean-revert, especially when liquidity conditions change. A hot NFP print this morning has rekindled rate-hike talk, and a risk-off equity move would quickly stress-test the new narrative. Watch whether BTC tracks gold or the S&amp;P on the next real macro shock.
