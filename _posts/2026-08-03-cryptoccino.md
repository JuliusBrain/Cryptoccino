## Coldcard Firmware Exploit Crosses $89M as Third Wave of Drains Continues.
{: #coldcard-firmware-exploit-crosses-89m-as-third-wave-of-drains-continues}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/hackers-target-bitcoin-s-safest-hiding-place-in-ongoing-attack) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/374817/coldcard-bitcoin-exploit-88-million-attackers-draining-wallets) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/coldcard-firmware-flaw-bitcoin-hack-89-million/) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/02/unlike-the-ftx-collapse-the-usd88-million-coldcard-exploit-has-investors-sending-bitcoin-back-to-exchanges)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">28<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Attackers exploiting a firmware vulnerability in Coldcard hardware wallets have now drained roughly 1,367 BTC across 4,585 addresses, with Galaxy Research confirming a third wave of thefts still in progress. Total observed losses sit at approximately $89 million and the attack surface remains open. This is an active, ongoing drain, not a post-mortem.

**Why it matters.** Coldcard has long been the device of choice for security-conscious self-custodians, so a confirmed firmware-level exploit fundamentally undermines one of the most trusted links in the self-custody stack. On-chain data already shows victims moving BTC back to centralised exchanges, a behavioural shift that could accelerate if the vulnerability is not patched and publicly disclosed quickly. With the Fear and Greed Index sitting at 28 (Fear) and trending lower, this is the wrong moment for hardware-wallet confidence to crack.

**The catch.** The full attack vector has not been publicly confirmed by Coinkite, and the scale of undetected exposure across older firmware versions is still unknown. Operators running treasury or multisig setups with any Coldcard device should treat this as a live incident: rotate keys off affected hardware now rather than waiting for official guidance. Scope could expand materially before a patch lands.
