## Coldcard Firmware Flaw Bleeds $70 Million in BTC as Loss Estimates Keep Climbing
{: #coldcard-firmware-flaw-bleeds-70-million-in-btc-as-loss-estimates-keep-climbing}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/post/410332/bitcoin-losses-linked-coldcard-vulnerability-70-million-galaxy-research) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/31/major-bitcoin-wallet-flaw-drains-594-btc-in-25-minute-sweep) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/374766/38m-in-bitcoin-drained-by-coldcard-key-flaw-its-maker-thinks-ai-found) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/31/coldcard-s-usd38-million-so-far-exploit-shakes-faith-in-self-custody-may-push-investors-to-etfs) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/31/live-updates-bitcoin-steady-as-japan-holds-rates-at-1-keeping-the-yen-carry-trade-alive)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">27<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** A vulnerability in Coldcard's open-source firmware allowed an attacker to sweep roughly 1,000 BTC across nearly 1,200 addresses in under 25 minutes. Initial estimates put losses at $38 million; Galaxy Research now pegs the total closer to $70 million. Coinkite, the device maker, has urged all users to move funds immediately and believes the flaw may have been uncovered by an attacker using AI to audit historical firmware releases.

**Why it matters.** Coldcard is widely considered the gold standard in air-gapped self-custody, so a successful exploit at this scale is a credibility event for the entire hardware wallet category, not just one product. The incident is already prompting discussion about whether retail holders will rotate toward custodial ETF wrappers. With the Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 27, the timing adds weight to the bearish read: the one argument for self-custody just took a public hit.

**The catch.** The loss figure is still moving. Galaxy's $70 million count may not be the final tally if the attacker had access to additional key material derived from the same flaw. The AI-assisted discovery angle is unconfirmed and comes from Coinkite itself, which has an obvious incentive to frame the attack as extraordinarily sophisticated rather than a routine oversight.
