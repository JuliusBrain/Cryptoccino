## Bitcoin ETF November Outflows Hit a Record $4.71 Billion as BTC Breaks Below $60K
{: #bitcoin-etf-november-outflows-hit-a-record-471-billion-as-btc-breaks-below-60k}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/bitcoin-funds-head-for-worst-month-as-outflows-near-5-billion) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-24-bitcoin-etfs-another-570-million-net-outflows-november-total-nears-5-billion-btc-dips-below-60k-445548) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/daybook-us/2026/11/24/bitcoin-drops-as-november-spot-etf-outflows-increase-to-record-usd4-71-billion) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/383909/bitcoin-losses-worst-since-ftx-is-bottom-near)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">13<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Monday alone saw another $570 million leave US-listed Bitcoin ETFs, with BlackRock's IBIT responsible for $509 million of that. November's running total now stands at a record $4.71 billion in net outflows, the worst single month since the products launched in early 2024. BTC slid back through $60,000 after a brief Jensen Huang-driven relief rally failed to hold, with liquidations tripling in the process.

**Why it matters.** At the $55,000 low hit earlier in the week, 11.6 million BTC was sitting underwater — a record by address count. Realised losses nearly doubled in a week, a pace Glassnode says resembles prior cycle bottoms, though another leg to around $53,200 remains on the table. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 13, deep in extreme fear, and the seven-day delta is barely positive, meaning the panic has not meaningfully abated.

**The catch.** Options desks are reporting put-buying at a pace last seen in 2022, which can be a capitulation signal but also a self-fulfilling one if spot holders read it as permission to sell. The macro headwind is real: tariff noise has re-entered the picture and the AI trade that briefly lifted risk assets did not carry crypto with it. BTCS, a Nasdaq-listed Ethereum treasury firm, was forced to dump 27,300 ETH to repay $43.8 million in Aave debt — a visible forced seller in a thin market.
