## Spy chip inside counterfeit Ledger units drained recovery phrases over LTE, with $86 million suspected lost.
{: #spy-chip-inside-counterfeit-ledger-units-drained-recovery-phrases-over-lte-with-86-million-suspected-lost}

[`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380826/ledger-finds-lte-implant-that-stole-recovery-phrases-in-suspected-86m-drain) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-13-ledger-confirms-hardware-implant-cryptobilis-reseller-units-mediamarkt-suspends-sales-europe-418442) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/13/ledger-says-spy-chip-inside-cryptobilis-ledger-devices-transmitted-wallet-keys-over-mobile-network) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380848/trezor-cuts-resellers-ledger-finds-spy-implant-crypto-wallets)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">62<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−9 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Ledger confirmed that devices sold by the CryptoBilis reseller contained a rogue LTE-capable chip soldered into the hardware. During initial setup, the implant read the 24-word recovery phrase off the display and transmitted it externally, leaving Ledger's Secure Element and firmware entirely untouched and offering no on-device signal of compromise. Suspected losses are around $86 million. MediaMarkt has suspended Ledger sales across Europe pending further investigation.

**Why it matters.** This is a supply-chain attack on the physical layer, bypassing every software security assumption the hardware wallet industry is built on. Self-custody's core value proposition is intact only if the device itself is trustworthy, and this incident proves that reseller channels are now a credible attack surface at scale. Trezor has responded by announcing it will terminate all third-party reseller authorisations from 1 January 2027.

**The catch.** Devices purchased directly from Ledger's own store are not implicated, but the CryptoBilis units were indistinguishable from genuine hardware to the naked eye. Operators and fund custodians sourcing hardware wallets through secondary or grey-market channels should treat any device not ordered directly from the manufacturer as potentially compromised and rotate seeds accordingly. The broader question of how this passed undetected across MediaMarkt's European shelf stock for long enough to accumulate $86 million in losses is unanswered.
