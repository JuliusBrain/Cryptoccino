## Bitcoin ETFs Flip Negative for 2026 as Altcoins Shed $200 Billion and Sentiment Craters.
{: #bitcoin-etfs-flip-negative-for-2026-as-altcoins-shed-200-billion-and-sentiment-craters}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-07-bitcoin-etfs-flip-negative-for-2026-after-1-28-billion-outflow-week-worst-since-june-435984) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/altcoins-lose-200-billion-moving-average/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">28<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−6 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Spot Bitcoin ETFs posted their worst week of outflows since June, losing $1.28 billion in aggregate and dragging year-to-date flows into negative territory. BlackRock's IBIT alone shed $403 million on Friday, accounting for 98% of that day's total. Simultaneously, the altcoin market dropped roughly $200 billion in market cap, with 70% of tracked tokens falling below their 200-day moving averages.

**Why it matters.** ETF flows had been the primary narrative underpinning institutional conviction in this cycle. Turning negative for the full year removes that talking point precisely when broader risk appetite is under pressure from AI equity carnage and rising Treasury yields. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 28, down six points over the past week, reflects fear deepening rather than a contrarian flush.

**The catch.** Hyperliquid whale data shows BTC short positioning at the highest level since October 2025, so a squeeze remains mechanically possible. But with the macro backdrop deteriorating, 5-year Treasury yields at levels not seen since 2007, and ETF redemptions accelerating, the path of least resistance remains lower until a clear catalyst emerges.
