## Bitcoin ETFs post their worst single-day bleed of October, erasing the prior session's gains in one move.
{: #bitcoin-etfs-post-their-worst-single-day-bleed-of-october-erasing-the-prior-sessions-gains-in-one-move}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-23-bitcoin-etfs-swing-back-to-net-outflows-erase-previous-day-inflows-426483) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381700/bitcoin-etfs-bleed-october-turns-red-again)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">51<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Neutral</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $635.6 million in net outflows on Thursday, their largest single-day exit this month. BlackRock's IBIT alone accounted for $254 million of that, with four other funds also in the red. Ethereum and Solana ETFs bled alongside, leaving October's cumulative ETF flow at roughly negative $176 million.

**Why it matters.** The scale of the reversal matters structurally: institutional wrappers that were supposed to smooth out retail panic are themselves driving the exit. Bitcoin is holding above $81,000 for now, but the Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at a tepid 51, down four points on the week, suggesting the bid is thin rather than resilient.

**The catch.** Spot price has not collapsed in line with the flow data, which points to either OTC absorption or futures-side positioning cushioning the fall. CFTC data shows speculators net long just 2,418 BTC contracts, a cautious posture that leaves little ammunition if ETF outflows persist into next week.
