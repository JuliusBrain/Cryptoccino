**What happened.** August core CPI came in at 0.3% month-on-month, a tick above consensus, pushing Fed hike odds to roughly 70% and sending Bitcoin through $77,000 before a partial recovery to around $77,300. Zcash was the session's worst performer as leveraged longs unwound sharply, with the broader market selling off in sympathy before Ether liquidations of bearish bets provided a brief technical bounce.

**Why it matters.** Markets are now pricing a series of hikes through mid-2026 according to WSJ reporting, a materially different rate path than was assumed just weeks ago. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 63 (Greed) but has shed 10 points over the past week, a sign that sentiment was already softening before today's print confirmed the macro headwind.

**The catch.** Bitcoin's much-discussed golden cross has now effectively failed to deliver, with price pulling back on the very signal technicians were flagging as bullish. The divergence between a hotter monthly core print and a continued annual easing trend gives the Fed political cover either way, so the next meeting is genuinely live rather than ceremonial.
