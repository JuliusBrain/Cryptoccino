## Bitcoin Clears $85K on a Short Squeeze, Then Pauses. The Leverage Question Is Just Beginning.
{: #bitcoin-clears-85k-on-a-short-squeeze-then-pauses-the-leverage-question-is-just-beginning}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/bitcoin-hits-usd85-000-as-short-squeeze-forces-out-usd648-million-of-bearish-bets) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-21-bitcoin-taps-85000-for-first-time-since-january-as-crypto-short-liquidations-surge-415905) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378729/bitcoin-tops-85k-as-648m-in-crypto-shorts-liquidated) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/bitcoin-retreats-from-eight-month-high-after-dizzying-13-rally) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/billion-crypto-liquidations-short-squeeze/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">78<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+9 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin broke through $85,000 for the first time since January, briefly touching $87,000, as a short squeeze flushed out more than $648 million in bearish positions across the market. Total crypto liquidations over 24 hours topped $1 billion, with shorts accounting for the bulk. The move extended a 13% four-day rally before BTC pulled back in Asian trading on Tuesday.

**Why it matters.** The squeeze cleared a meaningful ceiling that had held for eight months, and spot buyers stepped in to sustain the move after the forced liquidations ran their course. Glassnode is flagging early altcoin-season signals, with capital rotating out of Bitcoin dominance into broader alts. That said, the Fear &amp; Greed Index is sitting at 78, deep into Extreme Greed, with the seven-day delta up nine points — not the backdrop in which follow-through is guaranteed.

**The catch.** Traders are piling leverage back on immediately after the squeeze, which is precisely what created the squeeze in the other direction. One on-chain trader, 0xc3ed, was liquidated four times in 14 hours for $32.5 million in short losses. The next three weeks of funding rates and open interest will tell whether this is a structural breakout or a leveraged overshoot waiting to unwind.
