## Bitcoin Slides to Three-Month Low Below $63K as $1.9B in Liquidations Accelerate the Flush
{: #bitcoin-slides-to-three-month-low-below-63k-as-19b-in-liquidations-accelerate-the-flush}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/14/bitcoin-drifts-back-to-usd65-000-deep-value-zone-says-analyst) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/bitcoin-tumbles-to-three-month-low-as-liquidations-accelerate) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/crypto-market-liquidations-bitcoin-slide/) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/15/xrp-crashes-to-its-lowest-since-november-2024-as-money-leaves-crypto)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">10<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−18 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin broke below $63,000 over the weekend, reaching its lowest price since mid-August and triggering a cascade of leveraged-long liquidations that pushed total crypto liquidations past $1.9 billion. XRP simultaneously hit its weakest level since November 2024, with broad-based selling across altcoins suggesting capital rotation out of the asset class rather than rotation within it.

**Why it matters.** This is the fourth analyst-flagged capitulation wave of the cycle, and it is arriving alongside two consecutive weeks of billion-dollar outflows from US spot Bitcoin ETFs, with roughly $1.55 billion exiting last week alone. The Fear &amp; Greed Index is sitting at 10, deep in Extreme Fear and down 18 points over seven days, the kind of reading that historically precedes either a sharp relief bounce or a prolonged grind lower — and right now nothing macro is set up to force the former.

**The catch.** BitMine chairman Tom Lee is pointing at the Anthropic IPO pipeline and the AI selloff following Jensen Huang's exit as contributing pressure, arguing that institutional capital is being pulled toward AI liquidity events. If that framing holds, crypto faces a demand headwind that is not purely endogenous and will not resolve with on-chain data alone.

> **Options positioning may worsen any further move.** A CoinDesk analysis flags that derivatives structure, specifically concentrated gamma exposure near current spot levels, could amplify swings in both Bitcoin and Ether as market makers hedge. The dynamic is not new but is particularly acute given thin weekend liquidity and the size of the recent flush. [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/14/a-behind-the-scenes-force-may-amplify-bitcoin-and-ether-price-volatility)
