## Bitcoin breaks below $70,000 as Nvidia shock, rate fears and five straight ETF outflow days converge.
{: #bitcoin-breaks-below-70000-as-nvidia-shock-rate-fears-and-five-straight-etf-outflow-days-converge}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/13/bitcoin-dips-below-usd70-000-as-selloff-gathers-pace) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/bitcoin-slips-below-70-000-as-higher-yields-weigh-on-crypto) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-12-bitcoin-12-week-low-jensen-huang-exit-nvidia-438992) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-below-70000-liquidations-622m/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">20<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−8 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slid through $70,000 to a 12-week low, touching $69K at its worst, as Jensen Huang's resignation from Nvidia rippled through risk assets and the DXY hit a 19-month high on fresh inflation data. The move flushed roughly $622 million in leveraged positions across crypto, including $225 million in a single hour, and dragged Strategy and Metaplanet into their MSCI index expulsion at the same moment.

**Why it matters.** Five consecutive days of net outflows from US spot Bitcoin ETFs have now totalled $1.44 billion, suggesting institutional allocators are trimming risk rather than buying the dip. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 20, deep in Extreme Fear and down 8 points on the week, yet persistent selling rather than a reflexive bounce is what's characterising the tape right now.

**The catch.** SpaceX-Tesla merger speculation has re-surfaced a narrative around Elon Musk's combined entity holding roughly 30,000 BTC, which could act as a floor bid if the story develops. For now it's noise, not a confirmed bid, and a strong dollar plus rising real rates are the dominant variables.
