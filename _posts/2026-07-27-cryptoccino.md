## Bitcoin bounces to $65K on US-Iran de-escalation as oil takes a 5% hit.
{: #bitcoin-bounces-to-65k-on-us-iran-de-escalation-as-oil-takes-a-5-hit}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/27/bitcoin-is-back-above-usd65-000-as-u-s-iran-hold-fire-oil-drops-5) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/oil-prices-drop-as-us-iran-ceasefire-eases-supply-disruption-concerns/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">30<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin crossed back above $65,000 after the US and Iran stepped back from direct confrontation, removing the acute risk-off pressure that had weighed on the market through the weekend. Oil dropped roughly 5% on the same news, pulling commodity-driven inflation fears lower and giving equities a bid alongside crypto.

**Why it matters.** The move is a clean macro read: the correlation between Middle East escalation risk and Bitcoin selling held on the way down and has now unwound symmetrically on the relief. That said, the Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 30 (Fear), up just one point on the week, so the relief has not yet translated into any broad sentiment shift.

**The catch.** The Hormuz and Bab al-Mandeb straits remain partially restricted for tanker traffic, meaning the supply disruption is not fully resolved and any diplomatic relapse could quickly reverse the oil and crypto moves. Strategy is also four weeks without a Bitcoin purchase and sits roughly $9.3 billion underwater at current prices, which removes one of the more reliable demand signals from the board.
