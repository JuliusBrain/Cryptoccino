## Bitcoin Breaks Its 50-Week Average as $1 Billion in Longs Are Swept Out.
{: #bitcoin-breaks-its-50-week-average-as-1-billion-in-longs-are-swept-out}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-09-bitcoin-loses-50-week-moving-average-as-24-hour-crypto-long-liquidations-top-1-billion-436102) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382861/bitcoin-limbo-stocks-rebound-us-inflation) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/09/tech-selloff-weighs-on-crypto-majors) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/10/bitcoin-faces-u-s-cpi-test-as-analysts-warn-inflation-relief-may-not-be-enough)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">32<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+6 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin closed below its 50-week moving average as a Nvidia-led tech selloff dragged risk assets lower, triggering over $1 billion in crypto long liquidations in 24 hours. The coin was hovering near $75,000 while equities and gold staged a partial recovery, leaving crypto as the odd asset out. BlackRock moved $213 million in BTC and $118 million in ETH as ETF redemptions forced asset sales.

**Why it matters.** The 50-week MA is a widely-watched structural level; closing below it shifts the technical picture from correction to something more cautious. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 32, deep in Fear territory, yet is 6 points higher than a week ago — a small counter-signal that the worst of the sentiment flush may already be behind us. Analysts warn that Tuesday's US CPI print is the next binary: even a soft reading may not be enough to revive demand if ETF inflows remain thin.

**The catch.** MARA sold 13,597 BTC for $1.1 billion over the past six weeks to pay down $750 million in loans, and says more sales are coming as it pivots toward AI infrastructure. Strategy, meanwhile, added another 1,036 BTC at $82 million, pushing its stack above 850,000 coins. The two largest Bitcoin corporate holders are moving in opposite directions at exactly the wrong moment for price stability.
