## Oil Breaks $100 as Trump Suspends Iran Strikes, Dragging Crypto Down $80B
{: #oil-breaks-100-as-trump-suspends-iran-strikes-dragging-crypto-down-80b}

[`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/trump-pauses-iran-strikes-bitcoin-drops/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/saudi-arabia-strikes-houthis-oil-prices/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-stocks-stabilize-as-oil-prices-drop-on-peace-talk-optimism/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">26<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** After 13 nights of US strikes on Iran, Trump suspended military operations. Saudi Arabia simultaneously launched airstrikes on Houthi positions in Yemen following attacks on energy infrastructure, pushing Brent crude through $100 per barrel. Bitcoin fell 2.3% on the news and the broader crypto market shed roughly $80 billion in capitalisation.

**Why it matters.** Oil at $100 revives inflation anxiety at precisely the wrong moment: Fed Chair Warsh has already flagged persistent price pressure, and rate-hike odds for July are ticking back up. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 26, deep in Fear territory, and the seven-day trend is still drifting lower, so the macro headwind is compounding a market that was already fragile before the first airstrike paused.

**The catch.** The pause is not a ceasefire. US munitions stockpiles, particularly interceptors, are a stated constraint, and the conflict has already spread to the Red Sea and Caspian. Any resumption of strikes or a fresh Hormuz disruption would push oil higher still, and crypto has shown it cannot decouple from that kind of risk-off move in the short term.

> **Bitcoin ETF volumes hit a post-October 2024 low.** Weekly trading volume across spot Bitcoin ETFs dropped to its lowest point since October 2024, signalling thin conviction among institutional participants. Meanwhile, Ether funds have pulled in nearly as much fresh capital as Bitcoin products over the past three weeks despite holding only about one-eighth of the net assets, a ratio that is drawing attention from allocators watching rotation signals. {: #bitcoin-etf-volumes-hit-a-post-october-2024-low}

> **Upbit lists Morpho and Euler in the KRW market.** The South Korean exchange added MORPHO and EUL to its Korean won pairs on 25 and 26 July respectively, giving a large retail base direct access to two of DeFi lending's more established protocol tokens. KRW listings on Upbit have historically moved prices meaningfully on the day of opening, so watch for volatility around the EUL window. [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/upbit-morpho-euler-krw-listing/)
{: #upbit-lists-morpho-and-euler-in-the-krw-market}

> **Robinhood Chain's RWA TVL jumps fivefold as tokenised equity volumes grow.** Real-world assets on Robinhood's own chain have scaled rapidly as tokenised stocks begin trading in larger size, suggesting the platform's vertically integrated bet on on-chain equities is gaining traction beyond early adopters. The volume growth matters as a data point for whether retail demand for tokenised equities can sustain itself outside a novelty window. [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/25/robinhood-chain-s-real-world-assets-jump-fivefold-as-tokenized-stocks-start-trading-in-bigger-size)
{: #robinhood-chains-rwa-tvl-jumps-fivefold-as-tokenised-equity-volumes-grow}

> **BitMEX will cease operations on 23 September, ending an 11-year run.** The exchange that defined leveraged crypto trading in its era has set a firm shutdown date, and users are being urged to withdraw assets before then. It joins Dango's perp DEX, Odos, and Satori Finance in a recent cluster of shutdowns, pointing to sustained consolidation pressure across the derivatives and DeFi stack. [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitmex-shutdown-operations-cease/) [`cointelegraph_defi`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/dangos-perp-dex-taps-nearly-months-launch)
{: #bitmex-will-cease-operations-on-23-september-ending-an-11-year-run}

> **Sberbank targets a December launch for crypto trading infrastructure.** Russia's largest bank is building out the rails to offer crypto trading to its customer base, with a year-end deadline. The move comes as the EU simultaneously adds HTX and several other platforms to its Russia sanctions list effective 23 August, tightening the gap between sanctioned and unsanctioned crypto access points in the region. [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/25/russia-s-largest-bank-sberbank-plans-crypto-trading-infrastructure-by-december) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/post/409668/eu-adds-htx-to-russia-sanctions-list-barring-transactions-starting-aug-23)
{: #sberbank-targets-a-december-launch-for-crypto-trading-infrastructure}

> **Robinhood in talks to integrate Crypto.com prediction markets.** According to the WSJ, Robinhood is negotiating to offer yes-or-no contracts from Crypto.com's derivatives arm directly to its users, a move that would put it in more direct competition with Kalshi. No deal has been signed, and the talks could still fall apart, but the direction of travel is clear: US retail brokerages are converging on prediction markets as a product category. [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/post/409665/robinhood-in-talks-to-add-crypto-com-prediction-markets-as-kalshi-rivalry-grows-wsj)
{: #robinhood-in-talks-to-integrate-cryptocom-prediction-markets}

> **BlueNoroff compromises crypto wallets via fake Zoom and Teams calls.** The North Korean group has hit over 100 victims across 20 targets using social-engineering lures dressed as legitimate video meetings, stealing wallet credentials in under five minutes from initial contact. Separately, North Korea made a rare domestic move, arresting a group of hackers accused of laundering funds stolen from a local bank through crypto, suggesting internal enforcement is tightening even as external operations continue. The CLARITY Act is designed to draw cleaner jurisdictional lines between the SEC and CFTC for digital assets, and Fidelity's public endorsement raises the political cost of inaction. [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/fidelity-push-senate-passage-of-clarity-act)

> **Bitcoin Policy Institute embeds staff inside the State Department's digital freedom programme.** BPI and three partner organisations will place employees alongside State Department officials to work on issues framed around digital freedom and financial access. It is a quiet but notable expansion of Bitcoin advocacy into foreign-policy infrastructure, opening a direct channel to how the US frames crypto in its overseas diplomacy. [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-policy-institute-state-department-program)
