## FBI Pins $6.3B Binance Hack on North Korea's TraderTraitor as Exchange Phases Withdrawals Back On
{: #fbi-pins-63b-binance-hack-on-north-koreas-tradertraitor-as-exchange-phases-withdrawals-back-on}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/31/fbi-confirms-north-korea-s-role-in-usd6-billion-binance-hack-asks-defi-community-to-block-btc-eth-sol-addresses) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/31/binance-says-it-has-replaced-90-of-stolen-crypto-after-massive-usd6-3-billion-breach) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/31/the-usd6-3-billion-binance-hack-paradox-centralization-made-the-hack-possible-but-also-saved-usd3-billion) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-01/binance-to-resume-customer-withdrawals-in-phases-after-hack) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-31/rogue-code-2-test-runs-and-no-sleep-inside-the-binance-hack) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-31/binance-restores-90-of-stolen-crypto-promises-withdrawals-soon) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382306/binance-hack-everything-you-need-to-know) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/fbi-links-6b-binance-hack-to-north-koreas-tradertraitor-group/)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">34<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−20 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** The FBI has formally attributed the roughly $6.3 billion Binance breach to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, publishing a list of BTC, ETH, and SOL addresses and asking DeFi protocols and exchanges to block them. Binance says it has sourced replacements for around 90% of the stolen assets and began phasing withdrawals back on Sunday, two days after the exchange locked outflows entirely.

**Why it matters.** This is the largest single exchange breach on record, eclipsing the 2022 Ronin hack, and it directly implicates state-sponsored actors who now have a nine-figure war chest to launder. The FBI attribution converts a security incident into a geopolitical one, which is a different category of problem for regulators and counterparties. Fear and Greed sits at 34, down 20 points over the week, reflecting how severely this has rattled sentiment across the board.

**The catch.** Binance's ability to cover 90% of losses underscores the upside of centralised reserves, but that same centralisation was the attack surface. The attackers are already routing funds through Tron-based infrastructure to sidestep stablecoin freezes, per Chainalysis, which means a meaningful chunk of the remaining 10% may already be beyond practical recovery. Washington is watching: the hack has accelerated an already tense pre-election debate on crypto oversight.
