## Nvidia espionage probe and collapsed Iran talks send crypto into a $1B liquidation spike before a partial recovery.
{: #nvidia-espionage-probe-and-collapsed-iran-talks-send-crypto-into-a-1b-liquidation-spike-before-a-partial-recovery}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/09/bitcoin-drops-then-pops-as-nvidia-cheating-scandal-and-failed-iran-talks-trigger-volatility) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/1b-crypto-liquidations-bitcoin-ethereum-drop/) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-08-nvidia-perps-slide-jensen-huang-436047) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-perpetual-futures-fall-hyperliquid/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−8 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin dropped sharply before partially recovering after two macro shocks landed in quick succession: Nvidia's board confirmed it is investigating CEO Jensen Huang over an alleged relationship with a woman identified by US intelligence as a Chinese spy, and diplomatic talks between the US and Iran broke down. Around $1B in crypto positions were liquidated across the market, with Nvidia perpetual futures on Hyperliquid sliding roughly 3%.

**Why it matters.** The Nvidia angle is not purely a tech-equity story. Nvidia perps trade actively on-chain, its chips underpin a large share of AI-adjacent crypto narrative, and any prolonged boardroom uncertainty ripples into risk appetite broadly. The Fear and Greed Index is already sitting at 29 with an 8-point decline over the past week, so the market was not positioned to absorb dual macro shocks cleanly.

**The catch.** Bitcoin bounced after the initial drop, suggesting the liquidation cascade cleared enough leverage to find buyers rather than triggering further unwinding. Huang has apologised publicly and pledged to cooperate on national security, while Bill Ackman is vocally defending him. Whether the board probe stays contained or expands is the unresolved variable for the rest of the week.
