## Democrats fire back a counteroffer hours before the Clarity Act's cloture vote, leaving passage genuinely uncertain.
{: #democrats-fire-back-a-counteroffer-hours-before-the-clarity-acts-cloture-vote-leaving-passage-genuinely-uncertain}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/09/15/democrats-send-counteroffer-to-republicans-over-clarity-act-provisions) [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/democrats-push-back-on-gops-final-clarity-offer-with-counterproposal?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss_tag_regulation&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-14-where-the-clarity-act-stands-ahead-of-tuesdays-senate-vote-414720) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/bitcoin-rally-stalls-as-optimism-about-us-crypto-bill-wanes) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378125/clarity-act-crypto-ethics-trump-key-vote)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">69<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Democrats dispatched a fresh counteroffer to Republicans on Clarity Act provisions just hours before Tuesday's procedural cloture vote, rejecting what the GOP had called its last, best and final position. The sticking points remain the ethics rules around officials holding crypto assets and the scope of federal preemption over state regulators. A bipartisan group of 18 state attorneys general, led by New York's Letitia James, separately urged senators to vote no, citing concerns that the bill would strip state-level oversight.

**Why it matters.** Bernstein analysts had argued last week that any positive surprise from the vote was 'definitely not priced in', and crypto markets briefly reflected that logic when Bitcoin climbed toward $79,000 on Monday. The rally has since reversed, with BTC slipping back to around $77,800 as the Democratic counteroffer raised fresh doubts. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 69, squarely in Greed, which means a failed cloture vote would hit a market that has already leaned in.

**Watch.** Trump accepted stricter ethics provisions earlier this week to keep the bill alive, a significant concession, but Coin Center warns that the revised Banking Records and Civil Accountability language still does not resolve the core criminal law question. Wednesday's Fed decision lands immediately after, so operators are looking at a two-day window that could swing sentiment sharply in either direction.
