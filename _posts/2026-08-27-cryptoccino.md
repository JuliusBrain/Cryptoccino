**What happened.** Bitcoin ran into supply at the $80,000 level after adding 23% in seven days, consolidating just above $78,000-$79,000 as traders took profits heading into Friday's $6.4 billion options expiry. ETF demand remained steady through the consolidation, and analysts at Bitwise and Bernstein cited Treasury Secretary Bessent's fiscal posture as a durable macro tailwind, with Bernstein targeting $150,000 by mid-2027 and a cycle peak near $300,000 in 2029.

**Why it matters.** CryptoQuant puts $83,000 as the threshold where the 365-day moving average flips and the bull market gets its official label, so the next few sessions carry outsized technical significance. The greed gauge sits at 71, its highest reading since just before last October's $19 billion liquidation event — a reminder that positioning is crowded even as the macro narrative improves.

**The catch.** XRP's 44% rally has rebuilt leverage across altcoin books, raising the cost of any broad correction. The options expiry on Friday will concentrate gamma exposure and could amplify a move in either direction, particularly if macro data — gold down 1% on stronger inflation prints — starts leaking into crypto sentiment.
