## Bitcoin and ether ETFs log $1.1B inflow week — their best since April — with the Coldcard hack quietly driving custody fears into ETF demand.
{: #bitcoin-and-ether-etfs-log-11b-inflow-week-their-best-since-april-with-the-coldcard-hack-quietly-driving-custody-fears-into-etf-demand}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-08-08-bitcoin-ether-etfs-draw-1-1-billion-in-best-inflow-week-since-april-despite-low-volume-411204) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/bitcoin-btc-etf-inflows-hit-850-million-after-coldcard-wallet-hack) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/375192/bitcoin-etfs-draw-854m-over-five-days-as-rate-hike-bets-fade) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/375194/morning-minute-bitcoin-etfs-see-biggest-inflows-since-may)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US-listed bitcoin and ether ETFs combined for roughly $1.1 billion in net inflows last week, the strongest five-day run since April. Bloomberg analyst Eric Balchunas directly linked the sustained bid to the Coldcard wallet exploit, with several bitcoin funds posting inflows on every day since the hack broke. Daily totals tapered toward the end of the week, but the streak held.

**Why it matters.** The Coldcard incident appears to have accelerated a custody-risk narrative that pushes retail and semi-institutional money toward regulated wrappers rather than self-custody. That is an odd tailwind: fear driving inflows. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 29, deep in Fear territory, yet the ETF bid is the strongest in months — exactly the kind of counter-trend that tends to precede short squeezes.

**The catch.** BlackRock flagged that bitcoin is decoupling from equities, framing it as healthy diversification, but that narrative only holds if spot price follows the ETF money. Bitcoin is still pinned just above $65,000, volatility is compressing, and put premiums remain elevated — the market is buying the wrapper without fully committing to the asset. CME data showing hedge funds flipping from structural shorts to net longs adds conviction to the bull case, but conviction and price are not yet the same thing.
