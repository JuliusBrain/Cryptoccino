## Bitget Loses $387M to Spoofed Internal Transfers as North Korea Shadow Looms
{: #bitget-loses-387m-to-spoofed-internal-transfers-as-north-korea-shadow-looms}

[`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379350/bitget-hack-387m-what-happened-why-north-korea-suspect) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/25/bitget-s-usd351-million-hack-happened-via-spoofed-transfers-not-private-keys-ceo-gray-chen-says) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/25/circle-and-tether-step-in-to-freeze-hacker-wallet-after-massive-bitget-crypto-heist) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379365/circle-tether-freeze-stablecoins-bitget-hack) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitget-hacker-withdraws-binance-transfers/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">74<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+3 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** An attacker drained $387.5 million from Bitget's hot and warm wallets by faking internal transfer requests rather than compromising private keys. CEO Gracy Chen said the method bears the hallmarks of Lazarus Group, though attribution remains unconfirmed. The hacker has since pulled $1.23 million from Binance and routed funds to attacker-controlled wallets.

**Why it matters.** This is the largest single exchange hack of 2026 and the first prominent case where spoofed internal transfers, not key exfiltration, served as the primary attack vector. It signals that operational security around internal settlement logic is now as critical as cold-wallet hygiene. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 74 (Greed), which means most of the market is not pricing in what a state-level actor running systematic exchange raids actually looks like at scale.

**The catch.** Circle and Tether moved to blacklist a wallet labelled 'Bitget Exploiter 8', but between them froze only around $318,000 in stablecoins. The attacker had already swapped the bulk of funds into ETH, which neither issuer can touch. Separately, the Bitget CEO herself disclosed an $80,000 personal loss from a Lazarus-linked fake interview scam, raising questions about the firm's internal threat-awareness before the main event.
