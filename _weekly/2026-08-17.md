    <h1>Weekly Recap — 11 Aug–17 Aug 2026</h1>
  </header>
<p class="weekly-intro">The week began with institutional positioning quietly shifting — hedge funds turned net long bitcoin on CME, and a string of traditional-finance acquisitions signalled that major players were committing to crypto infrastructure rather than circling it. By midweek the mood complicated: bitcoin flatlined below $65,000, miners sold heavily, earnings disappointed across several listed crypto businesses, and the SEC pulled its own rulemaking meeting with no replacement date. The most durable signal of the week was not in price action but in regulatory and structural plumbing: banks received formal clearance to trade crypto for customers, Tether submitted to a full Big Four audit for the first time, and Franklin Templeton secured SEC comfort for onchain cash settlement inside regulated funds. What shifted was the locus of consequence — less about where bitcoin trades today and more about what the rails underneath it are quietly becoming.</p>
