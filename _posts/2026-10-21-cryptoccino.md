## BlackRock Files Downside-Protection Bitcoin ETF on the Same Day IBIT Posts Its Biggest Inflow in a Year
{: #blackrock-files-downside-protection-bitcoin-etf-on-the-same-day-ibit-posts-its-biggest-inflow-in-a-year}

[`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381385/blackrock-files-for-buffer-bitcoin-etf-as-ibit-draws-665m-in-a-day) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/20/blackrock-seeks-to-shield-bitcoin-investors-from-losses-after-rival-fund-weathers-50-crash) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-20-blackrock-ibit-bitcoin-etf-665-million-421102) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381444/bitcoin-etfs-best-day-nearly-month-blackrock-does-most-buying) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/20/bitcoin-etfs-just-saw-their-biggest-day-in-a-month-thanks-entirely-to-blackrock)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">63<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** IBIT took in $665 million on Monday, its largest single-day inflow since October 2025, while every competing spot BTC fund posted net outflows. On the same day, BlackRock filed for a new 'iShares Bitcoin Managed Protection ETF' that would use options on IBIT to cap downside losses in exchange for limited upside participation.

**Why it matters.** The filing signals BlackRock is engineering products for the segment of institutional and wealth-management capital that will not touch unhedged BTC exposure — a materially larger addressable market than the base ETF already serves. Presto Research reads the inflow surge as positioning for a catch-up trade, though with the Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 63 and barely moving week-on-week, this looks more like deliberate accumulation than momentum chasing.

**The catch.** IBIT did all the buying while Fidelity and Grayscale products bled, so the headline inflow number flatters the market's breadth. Bitcoin recovered to roughly $83,000 after dipping to $81,400 during Asian chip-stock turbulence, and options traders were actively buying downside protection through the session, suggesting the smart-money read is not uniformly bullish.
