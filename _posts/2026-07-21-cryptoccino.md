## Bitcoin stalls near $64K as US-Iran conflict escalates and macro headwinds pile up.
{: #bitcoin-stalls-near-64k-as-us-iran-conflict-escalates-and-macro-headwinds-pile-up}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">25<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+3 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin briefly touched a one-month high of $65,700 before retreating under $64,000, moving in the opposite direction to equities for much of the session. The backdrop: ten consecutive nights of US airstrikes on Iran, tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, and a lingering AI-sector selloff following new Chinese model releases all weighed on risk appetite in crypto even as traditional stock markets edged higher.

**Why it matters.** The decoupling from equities is the tell. With the Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 25 (Extreme Fear) and barely off its recent lows, the marginal crypto buyer is not showing up despite two consecutive weeks of ETF inflows. Bitcoin is currently trading roughly 50% below its record high, and spot ETF flows, while technically positive, remain described by analysts as 'peanuts' relative to the prior two months of outflows.

**The catch.** A key volatility indicator is reportedly flagging conditions that preceded sharp moves in prior cycles, with some analysts warning of a potential 'volmageddon' setup. Hormuz disruptions affecting roughly 20% of global oil supply add an unpredictable macro variable that crypto has historically correlated with during sustained risk-off regimes. Resolution of the US-Iran situation, or further escalation, is likely to set the directional tone for the week.
