## Bitcoin Cracks $83K as Oil Tops $101 on Iran Strike Reports, Triggering $547M in Liquidations.
{: #bitcoin-cracks-83k-as-oil-tops-101-on-iran-strike-reports-triggering-547m-in-liquidations}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/07/liquidations-jump-to-usd547-million-as-oil-rally-drags-bitcoin-below-usd84-000) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/08/bitcoin-breaks-below-usd83-000-as-oil-jumps-on-iran-strike-plan-report) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380290/bitcoin-dips-oil-shock-rattles-markets-what-happens-next)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. **What happened.** Reports of a planned Israeli strike on Iran drove oil above $101 a barrel and pushed the 30-year Treasury yield to its highest since 2002. Bitcoin broke below $83,000 in the sell-off, with roughly $547 million in leveraged positions liquidated across the market in short order.

**Why it matters.** The macro correlation is back and it bit hard: crypto traded as a risk-off casualty, not a hedge. Worth noting that the Fear &amp; Greed Index still reads 64, down 10 points on the week, meaning sentiment was already softening before the shock arrived.

**The catch.** The daily chart structure, stair-step higher lows, is technically still intact, but the nearest cohort of underwater holders did not get the relief rally they needed. If BTC cannot reclaim $87,000 on the bounce, that cohort becomes a ceiling, not a floor.
