## Bitcoin ETFs flip positive for 2026 on $2.4B weekly haul, their strongest week since October.
{: #bitcoin-etfs-flip-positive-for-2026-on-24b-weekly-haul-their-strongest-week-since-october}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-26-bitcoin-etfs-turn-positive-for-2026-with-2-4-billion-weekly-inflow-their-largest-since-october-416944) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379391/bitcoin-etfs-notch-seven-day-winning-streak-as-2026-flows-turn-green)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">70<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Spot Bitcoin ETFs posted seven consecutive days of net inflows, drawing nearly $3B over the streak and erasing roughly $5.8B in year-to-date losses that had built up over the prior two months. The $2.4B single-week figure is the largest since October 2025.

**Why it matters.** Flipping YTD flows positive is a psychological line for institutional allocators who benchmark against calendar-year performance. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 70, firmly in Greed territory, yet only one point off last week, suggesting the inflow surge is being driven by conviction rather than late-cycle FOMO chasing.

**The catch.** The Clarity Act stalled in the Senate, so the regulatory backdrop driving some of this optimism now rests on administrative guidance from the SEC and CFTC rather than statute. That is a thinner foundation than the bill's supporters wanted, and it can be unwound faster than legislation.
