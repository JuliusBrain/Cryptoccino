## Solana's Alpenglow Consensus Goes Live, Collapsing Finality to 150 Milliseconds
{: #solanas-alpenglow-consensus-goes-live-collapsing-finality-to-150-milliseconds}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-10-21-anzas-alpenglow-goes-live-on-solana-cutting-finality-to-150-milliseconds-421508) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381484/solana-alpenglow-goes-live-in-most-ambitious-upgrade-since-ethereums-merge) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/21/solana-s-alpenglow-its-biggest-network-overhaul-since-2020-goes-live-wednesday)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">68<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+15 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Anza's Alpenglow consensus protocol activated on Solana mainnet, retiring TowerBFT — which had been in place since Solana's 2020 launch. Transaction finality dropped from roughly 12.8 seconds to around 150 milliseconds, a change the team describes as the network's most consequential upgrade since genesis.

**Why it matters.** Sub-200ms finality puts Solana into a different performance class for latency-sensitive applications: HFT-style onchain order books, real-time payments, and gaming settle before a user registers they clicked. For builders already on Solana, the change is non-breaking but immediately beneficial; for competitors pitching on throughput alone, the gap just widened.

**Watch.** Upgraded consensus under live load is where hidden edge cases surface. Watch validator uptime and MEV dynamics over the next 48 hours — the first stress tests at scale will show whether 150ms holds under congestion or slips back toward older benchmarks.
