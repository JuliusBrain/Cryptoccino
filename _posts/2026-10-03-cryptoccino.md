## Bitcoin tags $87K then fades as a weak jobs report scrambles the macro read.
{: #bitcoin-tags-87k-then-fades-as-a-weak-jobs-report-scrambles-the-macro-read}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-02-bitcoin-nears-highest-level-january-85000-sell-wall-clears-us-jobs-data-disappoints-417570) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/02/live-updates-bitcoin-gold-and-tech-futures-edge-higher-as-markets-await-u-s-jobs-report) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/02/u-s-added-just-29-000-jobs-in-september-with-unemployment-rate-rising-to-4-2) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/bitcoin-rises-for-third-straight-week-on-strong-etf-demand) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379936/bitcoin-heads-higher-fed-jobs-where-does-btc-go-next)

**What happened.** BTC briefly cleared $87,000 on Friday morning before reversing sharply after September payrolls came in at just 29,000, well below consensus, with unemployment ticking up to 4.2%. The $85,000 overhead sell wall had already been cleared earlier in the week, per Glassnode data, leaving the move technically unobstructed before macro reasserted itself. The result is still a third consecutive weekly gain, with spot ETF inflows of $2.65 billion in September providing structural support.

**Why it matters.** A miss this large effectively kills any remaining probability of an October rate hike, which should be constructive for risk assets, but the reversal suggests traders are reading the number as a growth scare rather than a dovish gift. Open interest jumped $2.3 billion this week and perpetual funding rates have turned meaningfully positive, meaning levered longs are crowding in at resistance. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 67 and has shed seven points in a week, a quiet warning that conviction is softer than positioning implies.

**The catch.** Bitcoin dominance is approaching 60% again, which historically precedes either a sharp alt rotation or a BTC-led correction that drags everything. CPI on 14 October is now the next clean catalyst, and between here and there the levered long book is exposed to any additional deterioration in macro data.
