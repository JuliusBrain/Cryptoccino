## Crypto market cap clears $3 trillion as spot BTC ETFs log their biggest single-day inflow in 11 months.
{: #crypto-market-cap-clears-3-trillion-as-spot-btc-etfs-log-their-biggest-single-day-inflow-in-11-months}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/bitcoin-leads-crypto-back-above-3-trillion-as-risky-bets-grow) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-22-spot-bitcoin-etfs-1-billion-daily-inflow-416005) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/22/spot-bitcoin-etfs-attracted-nearly-usd1-billion-on-monday-the-9th-largest-inflow-ever) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378921/bitcoin-etfs-take-nearly-1b-in-a-day-as-average-holder-returns-to-profit)

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+20 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US spot bitcoin ETFs pulled in $999 million on Monday, the ninth-largest daily haul on record and the largest since October 2025. Bitcoin is holding near $87,000 while total crypto market cap has crossed $3 trillion for the first time since January, lifted by a falling oil price, a record Nasdaq, and a Fed that hiked while quietly adding liquidity.

**Why it matters.** The inflow figure eclipses the entire previous week, when ETFs posted the weakest net flow of their short history. Average BTC holders are back in profit, which historically accelerates the next leg by pulling in fresh retail. The Fear &amp; Greed Index is at 71 — a 20-point swing in seven days — a pace that tends to front-run volatility as much as it confirms momentum.

**The catch.** Bloomberg notes leveraged perp positions are swelling alongside spot inflows, a combination that makes corrections sharper when they arrive. The unusual, near-identical repetitive trades dominating Kalshi's BTC and ETH perpetual volumes have drawn CFTC scrutiny, adding a regulatory shadow to a market that just reopened its risk appetite.
