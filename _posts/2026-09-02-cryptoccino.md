## Robinhood Chain's early revenue surge lifts ARB 30% while Bitcoin holds near $78K despite oil spike and Fed rate-hike bets.
{: #robinhood-chains-early-revenue-surge-lifts-arb-30-while-bitcoin-holds-near-78k-despite-oil-spike-and-fed-rate-hike-bets}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/01/robinhood-s-new-crypto-network-is-printing-cash-and-it-s-sending-arbitrum-s-token-soaring) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/01/bitcoin-consolidates-near-usd78-000-as-arbitrum-surges-30-on-robinhood-chain-revenue) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/377126/robinhood-chain-dex-volume-jumps) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-01-bitcoin-defies-oil-price-spike-and-rising-fed-hike-bets-after-best-august-since-2017-413218)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">63<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Robinhood Chain, the firm's Ethereum L2 built on Arbitrum, posted DEX volume of $1.6 billion with DeFi deposits and stablecoin holdings approaching $800 million. The revenue numbers landed ahead of most expectations and drove ARB up roughly 30% in short order. Bitcoin, meanwhile, held near $78,000 coming off its best August since 2017, shaking off an oil-price spike triggered by new Iran strikes and rising bets on a September Fed hike.

**Why it matters.** The Robinhood Chain numbers are the first hard proof that a major retail broker can bootstrap meaningful on-chain liquidity fast, which is a different category of signal from another L2 launch. ARB's move reflects the sequencer-fee economics: Robinhood volumes flow directly to Arbitrum's treasury. The broader market holding up under macro pressure is notable, though the Fear &amp; Greed Index at 63 suggests complacency rather than conviction, a thin cushion if Friday's payrolls disappoint.

**The catch.** September has a well-documented drag for Bitcoin, down in eight of the last thirteen years. Oil at a three-month high tightens the Fed's room to pivot, and the Iran-Strait of Hormuz situation adds a tail risk that is difficult to price. The Robinhood Chain revenue pop is early-stage; whether those volumes are sticky or event-driven will only be clear in a few weeks.
