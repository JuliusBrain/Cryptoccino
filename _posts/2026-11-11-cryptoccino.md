## Bitcoin breaks to a three-month low as hotter-than-expected CPI puts a December rate hike back on the table.
{: #bitcoin-breaks-to-a-three-month-low-as-hotter-than-expected-cpi-puts-a-december-rate-hike-back-on-the-table}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-10-bitcoin-price-cpi-inflation-73000-support-437301) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/bitcoin-drops-to-almost-three-month-low-as-us-inflation-dims-risk-appetite) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382950/bitcoin-falls-hot-us-inflation-fears-bigger-fed-rate-hike) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/10/u-s-cpi-rose-stronger-than-expected-0-4-in-october-core-rate-rose-0-3)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−5 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** October US CPI came in at 0.4% month-on-month, above consensus, with the core reading at 0.3%. Bitcoin slipped below $74,000 on the print, hitting its lowest level in roughly three months. Equities recovered partially; crypto did not follow.

**Why it matters.** Markets are now actively pricing a Fed rate increase at the December meeting, reversing the pause narrative that had been holding risk assets together. Bitcoin's failure to track the equity rebound is the more telling signal: the Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 29, down five points over the past week, and the correlation that made BTC a risk-on proxy appears to be fragmenting precisely when it would have helped. Analysts flagged $73,000 as the near-term support level and a possible slide toward $69,000 if that breaks.

**The catch.** MARA reduced its BTC holdings during the drawdown, adding selling pressure from a miner that has historically been a buyer of last resort. ETF outflows are also mounting, so the marginal bid that cushioned earlier corrections is thinner than it looks on the chart.
