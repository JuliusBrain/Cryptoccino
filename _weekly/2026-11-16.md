    <h1>Weekly Recap — 10 Nov–16 Nov 2026</h1>
  </header>
<p class="weekly-intro">This was a week defined by two colliding forces: a macro-driven selloff that exposed the fragility of leveraged treasury strategies and thinning ETF flows, and a simultaneous acceleration in the institutional plumbing being built underneath crypto markets. Tokenised securities found new legal homes, traditional banks committed real capital to stablecoin infrastructure, and regulators began filling the seats that had kept rulemaking frozen. The tension between a deteriorating price environment and a maturing structural layer ran through almost every significant story.</p>
