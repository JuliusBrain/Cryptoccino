## AI Stock Rout Wipes $1 Billion in Crypto Longs and Sends ETFs Bleeding.
{: #ai-stock-rout-wipes-1-billion-in-crypto-longs-and-sends-etfs-bleeding}

[`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382717/bitcoin-price-11-week-low-crypto-liquidations-ai-stock) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/daybook-us/2026/11/06/over-usd1-billion-in-bullish-bets-liquidated-while-etf-flows-suggest-no-help-in-spot-market) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-06-crypto-etfs-see-over-half-a-billion-dollars-in-outflows-amid-post-midterm-market-slide-435873) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/382733/bitcoin-etfs-bleed-430-million-tech-rout-tests-wall-street)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">25<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−5 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** A sharp sell-off in AI-linked equities dragged Bitcoin down to roughly $75,500, its lowest print in eleven weeks, before a partial recovery to just under $76,000. More than $1 billion in long positions were liquidated across crypto derivatives, and spot Bitcoin ETFs shed north of $540 million in a single session, with BlackRock and Fidelity leading the outflows.

**Why it matters.** The correlation between AI equity names and crypto risk assets is now undeniable: when Anthropic's IPO valuation narrative wobbles, perpetual longs in BTC go with it. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 25, deep in extreme fear and down five points on the week, yet the spot market got no relief from institutional buyers who had anchored the last rally.

**The catch.** October's US non-farm payrolls came in at 165,000, more than double the 65,000 consensus, which typically pressures rate-cut expectations and strengthens the dollar. That print arrived after the bulk of the liquidations, so the jobs data is a headwind, not a floor. Spot ETF flows will be the clearest signal of whether institutional demand has genuinely stepped back or is simply slow to reload.
