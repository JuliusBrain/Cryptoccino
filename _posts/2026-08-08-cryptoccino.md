## A shock July payrolls miss reshuffles Fed expectations and gives crypto a brief lift, but bitcoin's technical picture stays troubled.
{: #a-shock-july-payrolls-miss-reshuffles-fed-expectations-and-gives-crypto-a-brief-lift-but-bitcoins-technical-picture-stays-troubled}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-08-07-bitcoin-tops-65000-after-massive-surprise-us-jobs-miss-411154) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/07/the-u-s-lost-23-000-jobs-in-july-far-shy-of-forecasts-for-a-gain-of-80-000) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/375118/bitcoin-price-death-cross-jobs-miss-rate-hike-odds)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. **What happened.** US payrolls shed 23,000 jobs in July against a consensus forecast of an 80,000 gain, a miss large enough to push traders to largely price out a September Fed rate hike. Bitcoin crossed $65,000 on the print before fading, and broader risk assets caught a bid as rate-hike odds compressed.

**Why it matters.** A softer labour market removes the main justification for further tightening, theoretically supportive for risk assets, but bitcoin's response was muted relative to equities. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index sits at 30, deep in Fear territory, a sign that even macro tailwinds are not translating into conviction buying right now.

**The catch.** Bitcoin is still technically in a death cross and has been unable to reclaim $65,000 with any conviction while Middle East tensions, particularly Hormuz shipping risks, keep oil as a headwind. Whale accumulation of $1.2 billion in BTC alongside $750 million in ETF inflows suggests smart money is building positions, but that doesn't mean price follows immediately.
