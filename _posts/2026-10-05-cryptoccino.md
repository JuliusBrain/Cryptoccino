## Bitcoin Charges to an Eight-Month High Then Reverses, Squeezing $113M in Leveraged Shorts Along the Way.
{: #bitcoin-charges-to-an-eight-month-high-then-reverses-squeezing-113m-in-leveraged-shorts-along-the-way}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/05/bitcoin-zooms-toward-usd87-000-nearly-setting-an-eight-month-high-then-reverses) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/crypto-short-liquidations-113-million-24-hours/) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380007/uptober-starts-green-bitcoin-etfs-draw-134-million)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">70<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin pushed toward $87,000 in early Monday trading, approaching levels not seen in eight months, before retreating sharply. The move triggered $113 million in short liquidations across the broader crypto market within 24 hours, with European demand cited as a notable driver of the initial leg up.

**Why it matters.** The reversal from a key resistance zone matters more than the headline print. Spot Bitcoin ETFs had already absorbed $134 million in net inflows over the first two trading days of October, supported by a soft US jobs report cooling rate-hike expectations, so the structural bid looks real even if the momentum stalled. The Fear and Greed Index sitting at 70, Greed, while down four points on the week, suggests sentiment is extended enough that a failed breakout here could flush late longs.

**The catch.** Ether underperformed on the move despite beating Bitcoin in Q3 returns, and a separate data point shows ETH market depth has fallen below 50% of Bitcoin's, meaning thin liquidity could amplify any sell-off. The Q3 rally in ETH was built on a narrower base than the price action implied, and that dynamic has not changed.

Institutional desks running size in ETH should factor in wider slippage until depth rebuilds. > **Euro hits a 17-month low on Spanish political uncertainty.** The single currency is under pressure as Spain heads toward an election with an uncertain outcome, adding eurozone fiscal noise to an already complicated macro backdrop. For crypto desks using EUR pairs or holding euro-denominated positions, the FX move is a cost-of-capital consideration. [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/euro-hits-17-month-low-amid-spain-election-uncertainty/)
