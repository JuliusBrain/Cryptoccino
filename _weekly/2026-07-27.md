<p class="weekly-intro">The week's defining tension was legislative: the Clarity Act moved from plausible to stalled as ethics concerns over presidential crypto interests and Tether's reported lobbying influence drained Democratic support, and Senate Majority Leader Thune effectively conceded the bill would not clear before the August recess. Against that backdrop, institutional capital kept arriving — Citadel Securities made its largest-ever digital-assets commitment, AI-compute pivots reshaped the mining sector, and RWA trading crossed a structural threshold on Hyperliquid — while a steady drumbeat of bridge exploits and physical attacks on holders reminded the market that infrastructure fragility has not been resolved. The mood was one of maturing ambition running into unresolved plumbing.</p>
