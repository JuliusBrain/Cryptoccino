## JPMorgan Lands JPM Coin on Ethereum, Putting Institutional Dollar Settlements on a Public Chain.
{: #jpmorgan-lands-jpm-coin-on-ethereum-putting-institutional-dollar-settlements-on-a-public-chain}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-19-jpmorgan-jpm-coin-deposit-token-ethereum-418874) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/19/jpmorgan-s-jpm-coin-representing-dollar-deposits-is-coming-to-ethereum-bloomberg) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/381343/jpmorgan-jpm-coin-ethereum-bank-dollars)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">55<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−2 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** JPMorgan has extended JPM Coin, its USD-denominated deposit token, to Ethereum, enabling peer-to-peer institutional transfers within the EVM ecosystem including Base. The token settles around the clock, any day of the week, backed by dollar deposits held at the bank.

**Why it matters.** A globally systemically important bank running a live settlement token on a public chain is a different category of event from a pilot or an announcement of intent. It puts regulated, bank-grade dollar liquidity directly inside EVM infrastructure, which every DeFi protocol builder and institutional integrator needs to take seriously now rather than eventually. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 55 — hardly euphoric — which makes the timing a signal of strategic commitment, not momentum chasing.

**Watch.** The immediate question is whether other GSIB-tier banks accelerate their own deposit-token programmes in response, and whether regulators treat on-chain bank deposits as demanding new prudential treatment. Adoption at the institutional client layer also depends on how custody and compliance teams handle the operational shift from private ledger to public chain.

> **CFTC rejects CME's legal position in a fight over jurisdiction on perpetual futures.** The regulator formally rebuffed CME's argument in what is described as a consequential dispute over which rulebook applies to perp contracts, a product with deep roots in crypto markets and growing TradFi interest. The outcome will shape whether perpetuals can trade on US regulated venues and under what framework, affecting every venue and protocol with a perps book. [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/19/u-s-cftc-rebuffs-cme-legal-stance-in-consequential-fight-over-perpetual-futures)
{: #cftc-rejects-cmes-legal-position-in-a-fight-over-jurisdiction-on-perpetual-futures}

> **Blockchain Association and Coin Center both file GENIUS Act implementation comments, pushing for tighter definitions.** The Blockchain Association wants clearer rules on DeFi protocols and foreign issuers under Treasury's proposed stablecoin framework, warning that vague language risks regulatory overreach. Coin Center's separate filing makes similar points on the boundaries of who counts as an issuer and what activities trigger the regime. [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-10-20-blockchain-association-clearer-rules-genius-act-421092) [`coincenter`](https://coincenter.org/comment-of-coin-center-to-the-department-of-the-treasury-on-genius-act-regulations-on-payment-stablecoin-issuance-offer-and-sale/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/blockchain-association-treasury-stablecoin-rule/)
{: #blockchain-association-and-coin-center-both-file-genius-act-implementation-comments-pushing-for-tighter-definitions}

> **France moves a stablecoin tax amendment through its National Assembly.** The amendment targets certain stablecoin-to-stablecoin swaps as taxable events, with separate proposals on exit taxes and self-custodial wallet reporting still under debate. France is moving faster than most EU member states in layering domestic rules on top of MiCA, creating a patchwork that operators active across Europe will need to navigate carefully. [`cointelegraph_regulation`](https://cointelegraph.com/news/france-stablecoin-tax-eyes-self-custodial-wallets-exit-tax)
{: #france-moves-a-stablecoin-tax-amendment-through-its-national-assembly}

> **SEC announces a November roundtable on machine-readable data and superintelligence risks.** The 9 November session will examine how SI-era data requirements affect disclosure, reporting costs, and market oversight. It is an early signal that the SEC is beginning to frame its data and surveillance infrastructure questions around AI capability rather than just current technology. [`sec_press`](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-106-sec-announces-roundtable-machine-understandable-data-age-super-intelligence)
