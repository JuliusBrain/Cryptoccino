## Crypto sheds $1 billion in leveraged positions as macro pressure builds and ETH longs take the worst of it.
{: #crypto-sheds-1-billion-in-leveraged-positions-as-macro-pressure-builds-and-eth-longs-take-the-worst-of-it}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/09/ether-bets-were-wiped-out-at-six-times-bitcoin-s-rate-in-crypto-s-usd1-billion-flush) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/ether-liquidations-six-times-bitcoin-rate/) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380490/bitcoin-etfs-worst-outflow-since-june-uptober-red) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-08-bitcoin-q4-forecast-418052) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380462/uptober-spooky-szn-bitcoin-cuts-september-gains)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">59<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−13 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Roughly $1 billion in crypto derivative positions were liquidated in a single session, with ETH longs flushed at six times the rate of BTC longs. Bitcoin fell below $83,000 before recovering to around $82,000 after Trump publicly ruled out strikes on Iran, easing the oil spike. Spot ETF investors pulled $485 million in a single day, the largest outflow since June.

**Why it matters.** The macro backdrop is genuinely hostile: oil near $100, bond yields at their highest since 2002, and no Fed pivot in sight. The disproportionate ETH liquidations suggest leveraged positioning in ether was crowded and fragile heading into the sell-off. Analysts at QCP now frame Q4 as an $80,000-$90,000 consolidation range for BTC rather than a breakout setup, and the Fear &amp; Greed Index has slid 13 points in a week to 59, greed territory that is eroding fast.

**The catch.** JPMorgan estimates $50 billion has flowed into digital assets year-to-date, an annualised pace of $66 billion, so the structural bid is not gone. But ETF outflows at this clip, combined with leveraged longs getting washed out, suggest short-term participants are repricing the risk premium rather than rotating back in. The daily chart still holds key support, but the cushion is thin.
