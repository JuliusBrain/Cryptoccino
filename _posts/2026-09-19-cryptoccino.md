## Bitcoin Clears $80K and Altcoins Run as the Clarity Act's Collapse Fails to Dent Sentiment
{: #bitcoin-clears-80k-and-altcoins-run-as-the-clarity-acts-collapse-fails-to-dent-sentiment}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-18-bitcoin-reclaims-80000-solana-hyperliquid-rally-crypto-markets-shrug-off-clarity-setback-415523) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/18/layer-2-and-defi-tokens-lead-broad-crypto-advance-as-post-fed-hike-nerves-fade) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/18/bitcoin-weathers-september-storm-as-rate-hikes-and-clarity-act-setback-test-bulls) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378630/bitcoin-blasts-short-squeeze-crypto-liquidations)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+8 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin ripped roughly 5.9% in a single session, pushing back above $80,000 and triggering a wave of short liquidations. Layer-2 and DeFi tokens led the broader advance, with Solana and Hyperliquid both posting strong gains. XRP climbed 6.9% and is now within striking distance of a golden cross on the daily chart.

**Why it matters.** The move came despite two genuinely bearish inputs: a Fed rate hike still in the backdrop and the Senate's failure to advance the Clarity Act. The market read those as already-priced, which is itself a signal. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 71 and has risen 8 points over the past week, suggesting the bid is broad rather than a thin-order-book squeeze.

**The catch.** Corporate treasury demand remains notably soft. Treasuries picked up only around 5,900 BTC across the most recent quarter, and ETF flows were mixed on the day, with Ether and XRP products posting outflows even as Bitcoin ETFs pulled in $433 million. A rally built on shorts getting cleaned out rather than fresh structural demand warrants some scepticism about follow-through.
