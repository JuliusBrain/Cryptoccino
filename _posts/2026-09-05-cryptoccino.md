## Bitcoin's Fed-fuelled rally hits $81K, then a blowout jobs print drags it back under $80K in the same session.
{: #bitcoins-fed-fuelled-rally-hits-81k-then-a-blowout-jobs-print-drags-it-back-under-80k-in-the-same-session}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/bitcoin-drops-below-80-000-as-hot-jobs-data-spurs-fed-hike-bets) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/377414/bitcoin-slides-blowout-jobs-report-fed-hike-odds) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/04/bitcoin-clears-usd81-000-as-privacy-coins-lead-a-broad-crypto-rally) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/04/live-updates-bitcoin-etfs-take-usd731-million-their-biggest-day-since-january)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">73<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+5 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Dovish remarks from Fed Governor Waller lit the tape overnight, pulling Bitcoin above $81,000 and sending privacy coins, HYPE, and ZEC to multi-year highs. August payrolls then printed at 162,000, roughly triple consensus, pushing September rate-hike odds to 58% and reversing most of the move within hours.

**Why it matters.** The day before the reversal, spot Bitcoin ETFs logged $731 million in net inflows, their largest single day since January, which suggests institutional demand is robust enough to absorb the macro shock without a structural breakdown. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 73 going into the jobs print is a useful tell: the market was priced for good news, not a hawkish surprise, which amplified the swing.

**The catch.** One strong payrolls print does not confirm a hike cycle, and six years of Bitcoin data suggest NFP days are rarely durable inflection points either way. The more consequential signal is whether ETF inflows hold pace next week once the rate-hike repricing settles.
