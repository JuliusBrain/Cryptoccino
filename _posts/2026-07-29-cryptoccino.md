## Kospi Plunge and Clarity Act Delay Deal a Double Blow to Crypto Sentiment
{: #kospi-plunge-and-clarity-act-delay-deal-a-double-blow-to-crypto-sentiment}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/28/bitcoin-slides-2-after-u-s-close-while-korea-s-kospi-plunges-10) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/28/bitcoin-drops-as-south-korean-stocks-tumble-senate-shelves-crypto-clarity-act) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/374579/xrp-price-slides-senate-clarity-act-fed-rate-looms) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-28/crypto-s-clarity-act-faces-longer-odds-despite-industry-s-push) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/clarity-act-momentum-fades-crypto-regulation/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">29<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−4 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin slid roughly 2% after the US close as South Korea's Kospi dropped 10%, dragging risk assets broadly lower. Concurrently, the Senate shelved the Clarity Act, kicking crypto market structure legislation to the autumn at the earliest. The two hits landed in the same window, compressing any bid from the recent period of Bitcoin range stability.

**Why it matters.** The Kospi move was driven by SK Hynix and memory-sector names, which had a direct knock-on into Hyperliquid's equity-linked perpetuals, where SK Hynix perps flash-crashed to $900. The Clarity Act delay removes a near-term regulatory catalyst that BlackRock, Fidelity, and other TradFi heavyweights had publicly backed, pushing definitive US market structure rules further out. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 29 before these hits signals the market was already fragile, not complacent.

**The catch.** Crypto clawed back off its worst Asian-session levels once the stock selloff appeared contained to AI and memory names rather than broadening. SEC Chair Atkins also signalled the agency would draft its own crypto rules if Congress continued to stall, which is either a floor or a threat depending on which desk you sit at. The Fed rate decision still looms as the next macro pivot point.
