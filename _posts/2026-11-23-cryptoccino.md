## Bitcoin ETFs haemorrhage $1.79B in a week as junk spreads blow and macro turns hostile.
{: #bitcoin-etfs-haemorrhage-179b-in-a-week-as-junk-spreads-blow-and-macro-turns-hostile}

[`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-etf-outflows-blackrock-ibit-weekly/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-spot-etfs-669m-net-outflows/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/junk-bonds-biggest-spread-blowout-2026/) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/23/market-rout-kills-50-basis-points-december-fed-rate-hike-bets)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">11<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Extreme Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin spot ETFs, including BlackRock's IBIT, recorded roughly $1.79 billion in net outflows over the past week, with a single-day bleed of $669 million extending the streak to four consecutive days. Simultaneously, junk bond spreads posted their widest blowout of 2026, and BTC holders realised approximately $17 billion in losses over the past 30 days.

**Why it matters.** The ETF outflow pattern suggests institutional allocators are cutting risk in size, not just retail trimming at the margin. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 11, deep in Extreme Fear, yet the seven-day delta is barely positive, meaning sentiment has not meaningfully recovered even as equities futures tried to bounce, which makes a reflexive ETF inflow reversal harder to call.

**The catch.** The same market rout that is punishing crypto has killed pricing for a 50-basis-point December Fed hike, which is structurally dovish. If the macro read softens further, the rate relief narrative could return quickly, but for now tightening credit conditions and forced deleveraging are the dominant signal.
