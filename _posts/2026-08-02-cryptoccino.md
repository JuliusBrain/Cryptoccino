## Cold-wallet exploit balloons to 4,500 addresses and $89M as Coldcard attack vector remains unresolved.
{: #cold-wallet-exploit-balloons-to-4500-addresses-and-89m-as-coldcard-attack-vector-remains-unresolved}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/08/02/bitcoin-cold-wallet-attack-spreads-to-4-500-addresses-as-losses-near-usd89-million) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/08/01/how-bitcoin-cold-wallets-lost-usd70-million-in-an-attack-that-never-touched-the-devices) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/01/binance-founder-cz-says-diversify-your-wallets-following-usd70-million-coldcard-exploit) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/374810/cz-warns-bitcoin-holders-70-million-coldcard-wallet-exploit) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/galaxy-research-coldcard-btc-attack-1367/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">27<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** What began as an estimated $70M theft from Coldcard-linked addresses has now spread to roughly 4,500 wallets, with losses approaching $89M. Galaxy Research confirmed at least 1,367 BTC drained in attacks that compromised addresses associated with Coldcard devices without any direct physical access to the hardware.

**Why it matters.** The attack bypasses the core security promise of hardware wallets: air-gapping private keys from the internet. The vector appears to target the key-generation or backup stage rather than the device itself, meaning users who followed standard self-custody practice are affected. With the Fear &amp; Greed Index already sitting at 27, a sustained supply-side scare like this compounds withdrawal pressure across self-custody advocates and institutional holders alike.

**The catch.** The attack surface is still unconfirmed — affected addresses span a wide date range and the common thread beyond Coldcard association is not yet public. CZ's advice to diversify across multiple wallets is operationally sound but does nothing to address root cause. Until the entry point is disclosed, every Coldcard-linked wallet should be treated as potentially exposed.
