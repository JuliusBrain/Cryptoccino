## Bitcoin Breaks $80,000 on Debasement Trade as ETF Inflows and Short Liquidations Stack Up
{: #bitcoin-breaks-80000-on-debasement-trade-as-etf-inflows-and-short-liquidations-stack-up}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/bitcoin-reaches-three-month-high-of-80-000-as-momentum-returns) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/24/bitcoin-hits-usd80-000-for-the-first-time-since-may-as-crypto-recovery-accelerates) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-surpasses-80000-etf-inflows-short-liquidations/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">74<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+33 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin crossed $80,000 for the first time since mid-May, capping what The Block reports as the largest weekly dollar gain in the asset's history. The move was accelerated by cascading short liquidations and the strongest week of US spot ETF inflows in ten months, with total ETF AUM up roughly $23 billion on the week.

**Why it matters.** The proximate catalyst was macro, not crypto-native: the Treasury's $4 billion bond buyback programme weakened the dollar, gold hit $4,695, and bitcoin rode the same debasement trade. Ray Dalio's public endorsement of holding 'a bit of bitcoin' as US debt risks mount landed in the same window. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 74 and has swung 33 points in seven days, which is the kind of velocity that has historically preceded sharp resets as much as breakouts.

**Watch.** Fed chair Kevin Warsh makes his first Jackson Hole keynote this week, with futures pricing a September rate hike at 36%. Analysts are flagging the next pullback as the real tell: whether spot buyers defend a higher low or the move unwinds back toward $75k will set the tone for Q4. Exchange volumes doubled in five days but monthly totals still trail July, so depth is thinner than the headline suggests.
