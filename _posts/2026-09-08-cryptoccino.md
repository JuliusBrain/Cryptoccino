## Liquid Network's 'white-hat' actors return 3,400 BTC but pocket nearly 600.
{: #liquid-networks-white-hat-actors-return-3400-btc-but-pocket-nearly-600}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-09-07-liquid-network-attacker-says-they-will-return-most-of-4000-btc-after-bug-fix-413673) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/377600/liquid-hackers-return-270m-bitcoin) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/377528/purported-white-hat-hackers-withdraw-320m-in-bitcoin-from-liquid)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">69<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Actors who drained roughly 4,000 BTC from Liquid Network returned approximately 3,400 BTC after Blockstream confirmed its bridge nodes had been patched. The two sides negotiated via PGP-signed messages embedded in on-chain Bitcoin transactions, an unusually transparent back-channel for an incident of this size.

**Why it matters.** At current prices the returned funds represent around $270 million, making this one of the larger partial recoveries in DeFi history. The fact that Blockstream could negotiate a return at all suggests the actors wanted plausible cover as white-hats, but the nearly 600 BTC they retained makes that framing uncomfortable. Sentiment is sitting at 69, firmly in Greed territory, yet a $320 million bridge exploit drawing a shrug from broader markets says more about normalisation than confidence.

**The catch.** The ~598.5 BTC still outstanding is not a rounding error: at today's prices it clears $47 million and there is no announced mechanism to recover it. Blockstream has said the bridge is patched but has not disclosed the root cause publicly, leaving other Liquid integrators to assess their own exposure blind.
