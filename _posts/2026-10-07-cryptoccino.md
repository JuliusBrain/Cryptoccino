## Bitcoin drops nearly $2,000 in 20 minutes as Iranian tanker strikes send oil surging and $487M in longs get liquidated.
{: #bitcoin-drops-nearly-2000-in-20-minutes-as-iranian-tanker-strikes-send-oil-surging-and-487m-in-longs-get-liquidated}

[`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-06-bitcoin-slides-crypto-long-liquidations-surge-417882) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/07/bitcoin-dips-below-usd84-000-as-oil-jumps-on-iranian-tanker-attacks) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-falls-2000-400m-liquidation/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta flat">±0 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin shed close to $2,000 in roughly 20 minutes after reports of Iranian attacks on oil tankers sent crude higher and risk assets into reverse. The move broke below $84,000 for the first time in days and triggered $487 million in long liquidations, part of $555 million in total crypto wipes across 24 hours.

**Why it matters.** The speed of the flush confirms that leveraged long positioning had rebuilt aggressively during BTC's grind near $87,000, leaving the book brittle to any macro shock. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 71 with zero net change over seven days captures the tension precisely: the crowd was leaning greed while bond volatility had already been signalling stress.

**The catch.** Geopolitical oil spikes have historically been short-lived catalysts for crypto, and the structural bid from ETF flows and corporate treasury buyers has not disappeared. The real question is whether a sustained move above $4,000 oil reprices rate-cut expectations enough to keep risk assets defensive through quarter-end.
