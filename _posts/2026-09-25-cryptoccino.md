## Bitget Confirms $352 Million Breach, Suspends Withdrawals While Claiming Customer Funds Are Safe.
{: #bitget-confirms-352-million-breach-suspends-withdrawals-while-claiming-customer-funds-are-safe}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/24/crypto-exchange-bitget-loses-usd352-million-in-hack-claims-user-funds-are-safe) [`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/bitget-says-351-million-affected-in-breach-halts-withdrawals) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/379275/bitget-hack-183-million-crypto-exchange-wallets) [`theblock`](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-24-more-than-170-million-in-crypto-moves-from-bitget-wallets-unidentified-address-416345) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/bitget-confirms-over-350m-breach-and-temporarily-pauses-withdrawals/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">71<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+15 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitget reported roughly $351.6 million in unauthorised transfers drained from both hot and cold wallets across multiple chains, all within under an hour. The exchange suspended withdrawals shortly after and directed funds to an unidentified receiving address that subsequently began swapping assets. CEO Gracy Chen issued a statement asserting that customer funds remain fully protected.

**Why it matters.** At $352 million, this is one of the larger CEX breaches on record and lands at a moment when the Fear and Greed Index sits at 71, a 15-point jump in seven days. Retail confidence built on that greed could unravel quickly if Bitget's 'funds are safe' assurances prove hollow. The speed of the drain and the multi-chain coordination suggest a sophisticated, possibly insider-adjacent, operation rather than an opportunistic hot-wallet grab.

**The catch.** Bitget has not published a verified on-chain breakdown of reserves versus the claimed loss figure, and the receiving address has already been actively swapping, which typically signals a motivated attacker moving quickly to obscure trail. Withdrawal resumption timelines and any third-party audit commitment are both absent from the initial disclosure.
