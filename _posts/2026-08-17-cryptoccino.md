## US Banks Win Formal Clearance to Trade Crypto on Behalf of Customers
{: #us-banks-win-formal-clearance-to-trade-crypto-on-behalf-of-customers}

[`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-banks-permitted-buy-sell-crypto/) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/08/13/the-long-bitcoin-short-the-bankers-era-is-officially-over-as-tradfi-giants-embrace-digital-assets)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">31<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US banking regulators have formally permitted chartered banks to buy and sell digital assets on behalf of customers, removing the prior guidance that had kept most institutions on the sidelines. The authorisation applies to custody and brokerage-style execution, meaning large retail banks can now plug crypto directly into existing account infrastructure.

**Why it matters.** This is the structural shift that crypto-native firms have been pricing in for two years: distribution through tens of thousands of bank branches and existing KYC rails, with deposit insurance framing nearby. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sitting at 31 suggests the market hasn't fully digested what expanded distribution demand means for liquidity, which is either a lag or a warning that macro headwinds swamp the news.

**Watch.** The fight that follows will be over yield. Banks are already lobbying against stablecoin products that pass interest to holders, and this clearance does nothing to resolve that standoff. Expect the stablecoin yield debate to sharpen now that banks have a direct stake in how crypto products are structured and priced on their platforms.
