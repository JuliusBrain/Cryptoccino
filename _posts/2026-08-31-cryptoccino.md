## Bitcoin Holds Steady as US Strikes on Iran Send Oil Surging and Equities Lower.
{: #bitcoin-holds-steady-as-us-strikes-on-iran-send-oil-surging-and-equities-lower}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/31/bitcoin-barely-blinks-as-u-s-hits-iran-sending-oil-higher-and-stocks-lower) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-attack-on-iranian-island-sends-oil-prices-surging-amid-rising-tensions/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/oil-prices-climb-1-after-us-strike-on-iranian-launchers-in-persian-gulf/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">62<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−11 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US forces struck Iranian missile launchers in the Persian Gulf and hit targets on Kharg Island, Iran's primary oil export hub. Oil climbed roughly 1% on the news, a supertanker caught fire after hitting naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, and Iran's forces claimed a drone strike on UAE's Al Minhad air base. Equity markets moved lower on the escalation.

**Why it matters.** Bitcoin near $79,000 barely reacted, a notable decoupling from traditional risk assets at a moment of genuine macro stress. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 62 but has shed 11 points over the past week, suggesting the greed reading is softening precisely as geopolitical risk accelerates. If BTC continues to absorb this kind of news without flinching, it reinforces the uncorrelated-asset narrative that institutional allocators have been pitching.

**Watch.** A prolonged Strait of Hormuz disruption would spike energy costs globally, feeding into the rate-hike expectations already pressuring EM currencies. The Fed's next read on inflation would arrive into a far noisier macro environment, and that is where the risk-off impulse could eventually catch up with crypto. Bank of America's reported $3.2B inflow into crypto funds this week is the tailwind; a sustained oil shock is the headwind.
