## Bitcoin clears a historically significant resistance level as altcoins finally catch a bid.
{: #bitcoin-clears-a-historically-significant-resistance-level-as-altcoins-finally-catch-a-bid}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/bitcoin-rises-above-usd81-000-while-near-jumps-23-on-zcash-swap-traffic) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/bitcoin-s-price-has-cleared-a-key-hurdle-that-has-historically-preceded-major-bull-runs) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/378695/holding-anything-but-bitcoin-losing-bet)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">70<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+13 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Bitcoin pushed above $81,000 overnight, breaking through a technical level that has historically preceded sustained upward moves. NEAR surged 23% on the back of elevated Zcash swap traffic routing through its network, marking one of the more meaningful altcoin prints in weeks.

**Why it matters.** The structural break matters more than the nominal price. A Glassnode and Bybit report published this week frames the past two years as a Bitcoin-dominant cycle, with altcoins largely giving back gains, so any sustained rotation into L1s would be a meaningful regime shift. The Fear and Greed Index sits at 70 and has climbed 13 points in a week, which is the kind of swift sentiment swing that tends to bring both momentum buyers and short-covering in equal measure.

**The catch.** Traders spent most of the week positioned for a washout, and the bounce has caught many offside. A move driven partly by short-covering and surprise Zcash routing activity on NEAR is a thinner foundation than organic demand. Watch whether open interest builds or whether this is a squeeze that fades into the weekend.
