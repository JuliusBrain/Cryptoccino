## Tampered Hardware Wallets Sold Through Malaysian Reseller CryptoBilis Have Drained Up to $93 Million
{: #tampered-hardware-wallets-sold-through-malaysian-reseller-cryptobilis-have-drained-up-to-93-million}

[`bloomberg_crypto`](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-13/crypto-wallet-tampering-fallout-widens-as-onekey-drops-reseller) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/12/ledger-says-unauthorized-implant-found-in-at-least-one-device-sold-by-cryptobilis) [`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/12/ledger-claims-cryptobilis-cases-are-limited-as-up-to-usd93m-is-traced-usd10m-frozen) [`decrypt`](https://decrypt.co/380706/ledger-says-all-confirmed-cases-in-suspected-87m-drain-tied-to-reseller-cryptobilis)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">57<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Greed</span> <span class="fng-delta down">−16 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** Ledger has confirmed that at least one device sold through Malaysian reseller CryptoBilis contained an unauthorised hardware implant. Investigators have traced up to $93 million in suspected losses to victims who bought through the channel, with $10 million frozen so far. CryptoBilis has stopped selling hardware wallets, and competitor OneKey has now revoked the firm's reseller authorisation entirely.

**Why it matters.** This is the first publicly confirmed case of a physical supply-chain implant in a major hardware wallet line, and it shifts the threat model for anyone who did not buy direct from the manufacturer. Ledger says its own systems and direct-sale channels are unaffected, but that framing does little for the victims, and the $93 million exposure figure is still growing as investigators trace wallets.

**The catch.** Ledger is describing confirmed cases as "limited", which is doing a lot of work given the scale of funds traced. The $10 million frozen versus $93 million tracked ratio suggests most of the money is already in motion through mixers or OTC desks. Anyone who acquired a Ledger or OneKey device through a third-party reseller, particularly in Southeast Asia, should treat it as compromised until verified.
