<p class="weekly-intro">The week's dominant thread was institutional infrastructure maturing under macro stress: spot ETFs bled steadily, a supply-chain attack on hardware wallets dragged on through multiple news cycles, and several TradFi-grade moves — from BNY's tokenised repo to Deutsche Bank's custody launch — signalled that the integration with traditional finance is now operational rather than aspirational. Bitcoin held a narrow range between $81,000 and $84,000, a price action that looked resilient given the headwinds but fragile given how much leverage was still unwinding. The mood was cautious consolidation, not panic, but not conviction either.</p>
