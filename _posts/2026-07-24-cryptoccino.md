## Brent at $100, US strikes on Iran enter night 13, and Bitcoin gets dragged into the risk-off.
{: #brent-at-100-us-strikes-on-iran-enter-night-13-and-bitcoin-gets-dragged-into-the-risk-off}

[`coindesk`](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/23/live-markets-bitcoin-trades-above-usd65-000-as-alphabet-s-bigger-ai-bill-props-up-the-chip-trade) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/brent-crude-hits-100-as-trump-reinstates-tariffs-amid-trade-tensions/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/oil-prices-surge-bond-yields-rise-amid-middle-east-tensions/) [`cryptobriefing`](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-military-conducts-13th-night-of-strikes-on-iranian-military-sites/)
{: .sources}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. Scale 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed)."><span class="fng-label">Fear &amp; Greed</span><span class="fng-value">28<span class="fng-scale">/100</span></span><span class="fng-class">Fear</span> <span class="fng-delta up">+1 / 7d</span></div>

**What happened.** US forces completed a 13th consecutive night of strikes on Iranian military sites as Iran rejected a ceasefire proposal brought by Iraqi intermediaries. Brent crude crossed $100 per barrel, bond yields moved higher, and US equities slumped on the combination of Middle East risk and renewed concern about AI capital expenditure. Bitcoin tracked equities lower, sliding from the $65,000 area where it had been consolidating.

**Why it matters.** A fifth month of active US-Iran conflict with no off-ramp in sight is a new macro regime, not a spike. Kuwait activated air defences, UK and German embassies evacuated Tehran, and the Strait of Hormuz is seeing its lowest tanker traffic since May. The Fear &amp; Greed Index sits at 28 (Fear) and has barely moved in a week, suggesting the market has been pricing caution for a while rather than reacting to a single shock today.

**The catch.** Bitcoin's safe-haven narrative is getting another stress test it is not passing. The correlation to risk assets is holding and the 'digital gold' bid has not materialised despite a genuine geopolitical shock. Grayscale argued this week that the four-year cycle is dead and BTC now follows macro and Fed direction, which, if correct, puts the next leg squarely at the mercy of oil, yields, and whatever Washington decides to do about Tehran.
