## Democratic midterm sweep sends Bitcoin to seven-week lows as the industry's political playbook unravels.
{: #democratic-midterm-sweep-sends-bitcoin-to-seven-week-lows-as-the-industrys-political-playbook-unravels}

<div class="fng-chip" aria-label="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index" title="Crypto Fear &amp; Greed Index. **What happened.** Democrats claimed Senate control and pushed hard toward a House majority in the 2026 midterms, triggering a sharp reprice across risk assets. Bitcoin briefly broke below $75,700, its weakest print in seven weeks, with roughly $250 million in leveraged positions liquidated. Nasdaq 100 futures slid close to 1.5% as Democratic sweep odds on prediction markets surged above 95%.

**Why it matters.** The crypto industry spent heavily on this cycle, with PAC Fairshake backing Republican-aligned candidates including Jon Husted in Ohio, who lost to returning Democrat Sherrod Brown. A Democratic Senate reshuffles committee leadership, puts the CLARITY Act's current form in doubt, and invites closer scrutiny of Trump-linked crypto ventures. The Fear &amp; Greed Index was already sitting at 34 before polls closed, down 17 points over the prior week. The election result lands on a market that was already leaning fearful, not one hunting for a reason to buy the dip.

**The catch.** Bitcoin ETFs had already shed over $1 billion across two sessions before a single ballot was counted, with the Binance hack the proximate cause and election uncertainty a secondary weight. That means some of today's selling is catch-up rather than pure political repricing. Coinbase's resumed staking in New Jersey after a state lawsuit was dropped is a small reminder that state-level friction can reverse without federal help.
